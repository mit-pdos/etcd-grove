# Track R12: txn, apply and lease without `threadTok` for mvcc (2026-11-07)

mvcc's API dropped `threadTok` from every contract (`b7b65691ff`; `notes/decisions.md`, "mvcc's
reader token carries the store's lock slots; mvcc's reads take no `threadTok`";
`notes/mvcc-api-invariants.md`, the hand-off of 2026-11-07). This track makes mvcc's clients in
`{Spec,Proof}/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/etcdserver/{txn,apply}/` and
`{Spec,Proof}/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/{lease,auth}/` build again with the minimal change: every
`threadTok` that was there only to pay mvcc is dropped; the others stay.

Every change below weakens a precondition (one resource fewer asked) and a postcondition (one
resource fewer given back) by the same `threadTok`; no other part of any triple changed. No new
`sorry` or axiom.

## Kept

- lease: `lessor.Leases`, `lessor.Renew`, `Lease.Remaining`, `leasesByExpiry.Less` keep
  `threadTok ∗ ⌜threadBound GF ≤ 2 ^ 30 - 1⌝`: they pay the lessor's own `RWMutex` reader pools
  (`lessorRdPool`, the lease's pool in `isLeaseObj`; `InvLessor`'s `.reader` mode), not mvcc.
- auth: nothing to change (it does not call mvcc; `NewAuthStore`'s `threadTok` is the backend's read
  pool's, `backend.bkRPool`, and only in a docstring).

## Changed specs (old → new)

### txn (`Spec/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/etcdserver/txn/`)

`Range.spec`, `Count.spec`, `Put.spec`, `DeleteRange.spec`, `Txn.specSole`, `Txn.specRead`: pre and
post
```lean
  threadTok ∗ mvcc.api.mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …
```
→
```lean
  mvcc.api.mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …
```
The restatements in `Proof/.../txn/Results.lean` (`range_rangeSel`, the `Txn` restatement) and the
lemmas `txn.end_read`, `txn.end_write` (`ApiLemmas.lean`: post `threadTok ∗ mvccReaders γ 1 [∗ …]`
→ `mvccReaders γ 1 [∗ …]`, as `Txn.End.contract`'s post) likewise.

### lease (`Spec/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/lease/lessor__Revoke.lean`)

`lessor.Revoke.spec`: pre `lease.lessorInv lp γl ∗ lease.lessorRevocable γl ∗ threadTok ∗ mvccReaders γ
1 ∗ …` → `lease.lessorInv lp γl ∗ lease.lessorRevocable γl ∗ mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …`; post `threadTok ∗
mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ lease.rdOwn γl rd ∗ …` → `mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ lease.rdOwn γl rd ∗ …`. The token paid
only `mvccDeleter`'s triple and `Txn.End` (through `mvccTxn_leaseDelete.law`'s `End` continuation);
`Revoke` takes the lessor's lock exclusively (`Lock`, no reader slot), and its other callees
(`Lease.Keys`, `sort.StringSlice.Sort`, `schema.UnsafeDeleteLease`, `backend.BatchTx`) take none.

### apply (`Proof/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/etcdserver/apply/`)

- `apply.applyTok E` (`Chain.lean`), the token every chain triple takes and gives back:
  ```lean
  def applyTok (E : AEnv GF) : IProp GF := iprop(threadTok ∗ mvcc.api.mvccReaders E.γ 1)
  ```
  →
  ```lean
  def applyTok (E : AEnv GF) : IProp GF := iprop(mvcc.api.mvccReaders E.γ 1)
  ```
  (the name kept, so no triple's text changed; every user of the thread token inside it was mvcc's:
  `newHeader`'s `KV.Rev`, `KV.Compact` and `KV.Range` in `Compaction`, txn's `Range`/`Put`/
  `DeleteRange`/`Txn`, and the lessor's `Revoke`). So every apply triple stated with `applyTok E`
  (the chain's, `applierV3backend.*`, the wrappers', `Apply`, `dispatch`'s, ...) is weakened by
  `threadTok` in its pre and post.
- `Contracts.revokeClause` (`isApplyLessor`'s `Revoke` clause): pre `{{ threadTok ∗
  mvcc.api.mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …` → `{{ mvcc.api.mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …`; post `RET #err; threadTok ∗
  mvcc.api.mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …` → `RET #err; mvcc.api.mvccReaders γ 1 ∗ …` (as `lessor.Revoke.spec`).

Proofs adjusted (no other change): txn's `Range`, `Count`, `Put`, `DeleteRange`, `Txn`,
`ApiLemmas`, `Results`; lease's `lessor__Revoke`; apply's `applierV3backend__{newHeader, Range, Put,
DeleteRange, Txn, Compaction, LeaseRevoke}` (the `applyTok` unfolded to the reader token alone) and
`LessorDerive` (`revokeClause` from `lessor.Revoke.spec`).
`Link.lean` of txn and apply regenerated (`etc/gen-link.py --txn`, `--apply`): unchanged.

## What the server must change (the next track)

`etcdserver/` outside `txn/`, `apply/`, `api/`:
- the users of `lessor.Revoke.spec`/`revokeClause` (`NewServer.lean`'s `h95`, `SnapApplyPost.lean`'s
  `hRv`; apply's `LessorDerive` already derives the clause without the token): stop passing and
  taking back `threadTok` around `Revoke`.
- every place that builds or splits `apply.applyTok E` as `threadTok ∗ mvccReaders E.γ 1` (it is now
  the reader token alone): `EtcdServer__applyEntryNormal`, `EtcdServer__applyAll`, `ApplyAllSnap`,
  `ApplyAllSnapOn`, `AdminEntryOn`, `SnapSendBranch`, `EtcdServer__snapshot`, `InvRestart`,
  `StopCensus`, `StopShutdown`, `NewServer`, `Results` (the apply loop needs no thread token for
  the chain any more: if its `threadTok` paid only `applyTok`, drop it).
- the callers of txn's `Range`/`Txn.specRead` (`EtcdServer__Range`, `EtcdServer__Txn`,
  `KVStoreRead`, `InvRead`): the reader token alone, no `threadTok` around the call.
- its own mvcc calls (`EtcdServer__Compact`, `__newHeader`, `__Cleanup`, `__LeaseLeases` specs, ...):
  per mvcc's hand-off. `__LeaseLeases`/`__LeaseRenew` keep the `threadTok` the lessor's pools need.
- outside the server: `examples/mvccrand`'s `writer__revoke` (uses `lessor.Revoke.spec`).

## Decisions

**The KV answers' revision reader** (`KVAns.revR`, what `KV().Rev()` needs when `fill` reads a
`0` revision): `threadTok ∗ mvcc.api.mvccReaders E.γ 1` (`KV.Rev.contract`'s), in place of `lean`'s
`mvcc.storeReader E.s E.γ` (the merge had `threadTok` alone, false: `Rev()` needs the reader token).
The same pair replaces `lean`'s `storeReader` everywhere in the scope (the lease-read contract's
`R`, the client's run slot `etcdRunSlot`, `ServeFromStart`'s slots, `AuthnServedOn`'s
`etcdGrpcSlot_auth(n)`), as R3 did in the server's `srvRdCall` (`threadTok ∗ mvccReaders E.γ 1`
where `lean` had the store reader): a handler's run slot carries the read's thread token and reader
token, beside the gRPC runtime's own thread token.

**The lease answers' revision** (`v3rpc.leaseHdrAns`, `leaseHdrOut`): the server's fact
`srvRevLb E (sint.Z r)` (`leaseGrantedResp`'s, `leaseRevokedResp`'s: a floor of the committed log
and the witness `□ E.Nx`), in place of `etcdReached (EM := etcdModelOfServer E) r` (over mvcc's
`certOk`). v3rpc's lease path is then mode- and model-free; the client turns it into its model's
`etcdReached` at the server's tie (`v3rpc.etcdReached_of_srvRevLb E γr on r hNx`, new in
`InvLease.lean`), so the client's lease handler lemmas take `(γr) (on) (hNx : E.Nx = srvApi.kvNx γr
on)` and state their specs at `etcdModelOfServer E γr on` (v3rpc's restated model).

**`leasesHdrDone`**: the header's log floor `srvApi.kvLogLb E.γs log` in place of
`mvcc.idxCert E.γ.idx log` (the server's `srvHeaderAt` already has it).

**`v3compactor.revLb`**: over `srvApi.kvLogLb γs` (was `mvcc.idxCert γi`; unused outside its file).

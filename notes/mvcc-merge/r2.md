## `revokeSize`: why `rqOk`

`revokePre` (ours') takes `(mvcc.stateAfter log).length ≤ 2 ^ 62` (the lessor's sort premise,
`lease.lessor.Revoke.spec`). Tried first: derive it in `dispatch` from the writer's facts. `dispatch`
has `envAus` (through `uberIs`), whose fact clause gives `⧖ (logCh log)` at the writer's half, and
`preceipt_lt` turns it into `logCh log < receiptBound GF`; `txn.stateBound_stateAfter` then gives the
bound from `logCh log ≤ 2 ^ 62`. But nothing in `dispatch.spec` (nor `Apply.spec`, `ApplyRec`, the KVL
variants) bounds `receiptBound GF`: deriving it there means adding `receiptBound GF ≤ 2 ^ 48` to
those specs (or to `revokeOwn`). So, as the plan's fallback: **`rqOk`'s revocation arm** is now
`revokeKindOk rq ∧ putOk log ∧ (mvcc.stateAfter log).length ≤ 2 ^ 62` (`rqOk_leaseRevoke` likewise),
a size clause beside `putOk`, which the server derives at its small log (`srvApplyKV_small`: `logCh <
2^48`, then `stateBound_stateAfter`), as it does every other size clause (`rqSmall_of_small`).

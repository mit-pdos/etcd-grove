## What is good

**`LineEffect` over every trace and global state (`:184-208`)** is the right revision:
- it reuses the per-line lemmas, which are generic in `t` and `l0`;
- the global step only instantiates it (`simRun_lineEffect`, `TraceSimMatch.lean:3382-3428`);
- F5 cannot recur, because an action on another server cannot produce `withNode σ i …`.

**`Chain` (`TraceSimLemmas.lean:2190-2196`)** is exactly what `stepLoop` needs:
- single successors, each fresh and satisfying the invariants;
- the receive lines' intermediate states, through `Mid`.

**The two laws are the one Iris law round 1 asked for (S1).** Their proofs are clean:
- the AU is opened at `⊤, ∅`;
- `simNS` and `raftLogN` are disjoint (`mask_ndot_ne'`);
- the abort path returns the share and the token;
- the commit path consumes the token (`ghost_map_delete`) and moves the share;
- the pending bag is moved by counts (`BagRep`, `flatMap_update_perm`).

`trCap_fire_recv`'s use of `MsgsUniq` is exactly what `LineEffect`'s `rcv` premise needs
(`TracedHooksGlobal.lean:158-161`).

**`simInv` parameterized by `Fx` (`TraceSimGhost.lean:216-225`):**
- it keeps `TraceSimGhost` free of raft's ghost;
- the bags are by `BagRep`, keys distinct, which `bag_count_without` needs;
- the phantoms have their own lists (`phP`, `phM`);
- `preVote`/`checkQuorum = false` are recorded once.

**`srvFacts`/`allFacts`** are per voter and persistent. A step of node `id` leaves the other voters'
facts untouched: `allFacts` reads `σ.s (idAbs j)`, and `withNode` changes only `idAbs id`. This
answers the brief's "other nodes' unchanged facts": they are free.

**The derived invariants are honest reductions:**
- `MoreThanOneLeaderInv` from `isLeader_unique`;
- `LogInv` from `isCommitted_agree`;
- `LogMatchingInv` from `plog_match_down` (`logAbs_match`, using the fact that an abstract entry is
  a function of position and term, `entAbs_eq`);
- `CommitOk`;
- `LeaderCompletenessInv` from `commit_in_leader`, whose three cases mirror `restart_ack_one`;
- `simFx_violated` assembles all eight.

**`SpecInv` replacing `DurInv` is a real catch** (`durInv_not_preserved`,
`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:1648`). The named hypotheses of each preservation lemma are the right shape
for a simulation to discharge. The problem is that `FxPres` does not give its prover what it needs
to discharge them (A2, A3).

**`MsgsUniq` and `QuorumLogF` are true of the traces raft produces.**

`MsgsUniq`: I checked each `Line.is*` predicate (`Trace.lean`) against `msgAbs`
(`TraceSim.lean:92-119`) and the specification's replies (`Spec.lean:394,424,455`):
- rejections carry `matchIndex 0`;
- heartbeats and heartbeat responses use the default fields;
- vote messages are fully logged;
- two term-`T` `MsgApp`s from one source with the same `prevLogIndex`, `prevLogTerm`, count and
  commit have the same abstract entries: they are slices of `T`'s leader log, which only grows
  while it leads, and `entAbs` reads only position and term;
- phantoms (lower-term rejections) coincide with real rejections when all fields agree.

`QuorumLogF` is `QuorumLogInv` itself (`Invariants.lean:66-71`), a true protocol invariant.

## First tier (wrong or unusable)

### A1. `PhaseStep`/`PhaseStepR` do not receive the configuration facts that `trCap` holds, so they are unprovable for the fixed-phase callers

**Principles:** hard rule "complete"; round 1's F4.

**Evidence.**
- `PhaseStep` (`:214-219`) is `∀ C cs P, Φ C cs P → …`, with no tie between `C` and raft's `c`.
- `trCap_fire` destructures `⌜tn.voters = C ∧ (elements c.incoming).Perm C ∧ c.outgoing = ∅ ∧
  id ∈ C⌝` (`TracedHooksGlobal.lean:219,222`), but calls `hps C cs P HΦ e HE` without these facts
  (`:265`). `trCap_fire_recv` does the same (`:359`).
- The effect lemmas need them: `lineEffect_msgApp` takes `hin : (elements cfg.incoming).Perm C`,
  `hout`, `hC` (`TraceSimMatch.lean`, after `:3440`).
- Without them, the line's `conf` (built from `c` by `NodeEvOf`/`SendEvOf`) cannot be shown to
  match the specification's `confAbs C` in `validatePostStates`. `LineEffect` is then false, and not
  vacuously so.

**Callers affected.**
- `traceCommit.tracedSpec` (`:411-418`) and `raft.maybeCommit.tracedSpec` (`:424-435`) fix
  `Φ = SimIdle C s …`, and `SimIdle` says nothing of `c`. Their `PhaseStep` is unprovable.
- `raft.send.tracedSpec`'s callers must thread the permutation through every phase they choose.

**Fix.** Give `PhaseStep`/`PhaseStepR` the configuration `c` and the trCap facts as hypotheses:

    PhaseStep id c E Φ Φ' := ∀ C cs P, (elements c.incoming).Perm C → c.outgoing = ∅ → id ∈ C →
      Φ C cs P → …

`trCap_fire` already has the facts to pass.

### A2. `FxPres`/`FxPresR` withhold from their prover what the `SpecInv` lemmas need, so they are unprovable for most line kinds

**Principles:** "a bridge is stated from its consumer's goal"; "every premise has a source the
client sees".

**Evidence.** `FxPres` (`:332-339`) gives its prover only:
- `Φ`, `E e`, `toC a = cs`, `Φ'`, `LineEffect`;
- `σ.server = confAbs C`, `σ.s id = a`;
- `simFx σ`.

Three things are missing, all of which the law has in hand when it applies `Hfx`
(`TracedHooksGlobal.lean:260-273`; for receive lines `:354-373`).

**(a) The received message in `messages`.** `FxPresR` (`:370-381`) does not say
`m ∈ σ.messages.domain`. Every receive lemma of `TraceSimSpecInv` takes it:
- `specInv_handleAppendEntriesRequest` (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:1602-1605`, `hm`);
- `specInv_acceptAppendEntriesRequest` (`:1561`);
- `specInv_handleAppendEntriesResponse` (`:765-767`).

The law knows it (`hmd`, `TracedHooksGlobal.lean:142`). For an arbitrary `σ` it is false, and then
`DurS`'s response clause and `LeaderEntries`' log clause for the receiver's new log have no source.

**(b) Pre-vote and check-quorum off.** `specInv_handleAppendEntriesRequest` needs
`σ.preVote = false`, `σ.checkQuorum = false` (`:1604`); the rejection is unsuccessful only then.
`FxPres`/`FxPresR` quantify over every `σ`. The law has `hpv`/`hcq` (`:260`).

**(c) Leader completeness and raft's vote table.** Several `SpecInv` hypotheses are leader
completeness:
- `specInv_updateTerm`'s `hcw` (every higher-term receipt);
- `specInv_timeout`'s `hcw` (`BecomeCandidate` lines);
- `specInv_becomeLeader`'s `hcw`;
- `CommitInSenderDurable` (`specInv_appendEntriesAlreadyDone`).

`allFacts_leaderCompleteness` needs `votesTbl γ`. That is a half authority in raft's invariant
(`GhostState.lean:735-737`), not persistent, so it cannot be used inside the persistent wand of
`FxPres` (`□ … -∗ simFx σ -∗ simFx σ'`). `σ.violated = .ok none` would give the guarded
`LeaderCompletenessInv`, but `FxPres` does not receive it either, although the law computes it
(`hv`, `:273`). `BecomeLeader`'s `hcw` (the candidate is not yet a leader in `σ`) needs `lc_core`
with the table itself.

**Lines affected.**
- every receive of an append request or response: (a), (b);
- every higher-term receipt, `BecomeCandidate`, `BecomeLeader`, `AppendEntriesAlreadyDone`: (c).

**Fix (the law already has every piece).**

    FxPres γ id E Φ Φ' := □ ∀ C cs P e a a' P' σ,
      ⌜… ∧ σ.server = confAbs C ∧ σ.s (idAbs id) = a ∧ σ.preVote = false ∧ σ.checkQuorum = false ∧
        σ.violated = .ok none⌝ -∗
      votesTbl γ -∗ simFx γ C σ -∗ votesTbl γ ∗ simFx γ C σ'

`FxPresR` also gets `⌜m ∈ σ.messages.domain ∧ 0 < σ.messages.count m⌝`. The law applies `Hfx` with
`Htbl` in hand (it is framed back after, as now).

### A3. `simFx` has no facts about messages, so `BecomeLeader`'s freshness and the receiver's new log have no source

**Principles:** "every premise has a source the client sees"; complete.

**Evidence.** `specInv_becomeLeader` (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:1148-1154`) needs:
- `hfreshMsg`: no entry of the new term in any message;
- `hfreshReq`: no append request of the new term in any message.

`simFx` constrains messages only through `SpecInv`. Its `CandS` covers only the candidate's own
requests, and `LeaderEntries` only terms that have a current leader. A term-`T` request from a
server that led `T` and has since moved on is excluded only by raft's `isLeader_unique` over time
plus a record that the message came from `T`'s leader. Those are raft facts about the bag's
contents (`isMessage`), which `allFacts` (logs only) does not carry. The same gap is in:
- `CommitInSenderDurable` (the sender's durable log against the receiver's commit);
- the pairwise slice agreement (`AppSlices`) that `MsgsUniq` needs once pending messages move
  (A5).

**Callers affected.** `becomeLeader`'s and `poll`'s hook path (`BecomeLeader` line), and every
append receipt.

**Fix.** Add a per-message clause to `simFx`, persistent like `allFacts`, keyed by the bag element:
- for each `m ∈ msgs σ` of type append request, the records of its entries (`logRecs` of raft's
  slice) and its sender's `isLeader γ src (term)`;
- with the commit's certificate (what raft's `isMessage` carries, `GhostState.lean:198,216`).

Alternatively, strengthen `SpecInv` with a pure "every term-`T` request or entry comes from the
server that won `T`" history clause. The first matches the notes' table (`trace-validation-sim.md`,
"The invariants", the commit-point row).

### A4. `raft.send.tracedSpec`, `traceCommit.tracedSpec` and `raft.maybeCommit.tracedSpec` take no `FxPres`, so they cannot use the law

**Principles:** complete; "a clause is stated over what its user holds".

**Evidence.** `trCap_fire.statement` (`:345-350`) needs `FxPres γ id E Φ Φ'`. The three specs take
`PhaseStep` (send) or a fixed phase (commit), but no `FxPres`:
- **`send`** quantifies over the caller's `Φ'` (`:513-532`). Its proof cannot build `FxPres` for an
  arbitrary `Φ'`, because the facts at `a'` depend on `Φ'`. So the obligation must be a premise.
- **The commit pair.** `FxPres` needs `QuorumLogF` at the leader's raised commit index. Nothing in
  `simFx` relates the leader's `matchIndex` to the followers' current logs. This is the open
  acknowledgement-history copy (notes, "The invariants"). Until it lands, the `Commit` line's
  `FxPres` is unprovable.

  The same holds at a follower's commit raised by a receipt, and at truncation intermediate states
  (`Mid`). The plan lists `QuorumLogInv` as open, but not that it blocks the commit path's
  statements already written here.

**Fix.**
- Add `FxPres γ id (SendEvOf evt id s c mm) Φ Φ'` beside `PhaseStep` in `raft.send.tracedSpec`'s
  premise. Better still, fold both into one Iris "phase step" (A8).
- Mark `traceCommit`/`maybeCommit` "blocked on `QuorumLogF`" in their docstrings.

### A5. `withNode`'s bag effect cannot express a `Ready` line, and `MsgsUniq` is not preserved by one

**Principles:** complete (the `Ready` law the plan lists next will need a different statement).

**Evidence.**
- `withNode σ i a' fM fP` (`:179-182`) applies `fM` to `σ.messages` alone and `fP` to
  `σ.pendingMessages` alone.
- `State.ready` (`Spec.lean:323-326`) sets
  `messages := (pendingFrom i).plus messages, pendingMessages := pendingMessages.minus
  (pendingFrom i)`. The new `messages` depends on `σ.pendingMessages`, including the phantoms from
  `i` in `phP`, which no caller sees.
- `LineEffect` (`∀ σ`) therefore cannot state `Ready`'s effect with any fixed `fM`.
- `MsgsUniq` (`:253-255`) is stated over `messages` only. A `Ready` adds pending messages, so its
  preservation needs uniqueness over both bags: `TraceSpecInv.msgs σ`, true by the same slice
  argument, with the current leaders' slice clause to maintain it at sends.

**Fix.**
- Generalize the bag effect to `fB : Bag × Bag → Bag × Bag`, or give `Ready` its own law. That law
  moves `pendingFrom i`, mints `inFlight` tokens for the share's `P`, and moves the phantoms from
  `phP` to `phM`.
- Restate `MsgsUniq` over `msgs σ` now: the `Ready` law will need it, and every other line keeps it
  trivially.

### A6. A receipt answered by a phantom reply has no law (round 1's S3, still open)

**Principles:** complete. Every lower-term `MsgApp`/`MsgHeartbeat`/`MsgVote` reaching a node
(routine after an election) is such a receipt.

**Evidence.** `trCap_fire_recv` puts all of the effect's `P'` into the node's share
(`A.insert id (…, P ++ P')`, `TracedHooksGlobal.lean:390`) and leaves `phP` unchanged. Raft sends
nothing (P7), so no `Φ'` built on `SimIdle`'s `P = pendAbs outbox` can hold. The phantom has to go
to `phP`.

**Fix.** Split the effect's reply into `P'_share ++ P'_ph`: `PhaseStepR` returns both, and the law
appends `P'_ph` to `phP`. This is a parameter of the one receive law, not a third law.

### A7. Laws missing for line kinds the workload produces

Beyond the plan's list (`Ready`, `BecomeFollower`, bootstrap):

- **Bootstrap-named lines past the node's segment.** `PhaseStep` requires
  `isBootstrapName = false` (`:215`). By P4 and `trace-validation-sim.md:337-339`, the application
  logs `ApplyConfChange` for the bootstrap entries *after* the node's first `Ready`.
  `bootstrapOf` (`Trace.lean`, `upper = firstNon - 1`) does not count these as bootstrap lines, so
  they must step `applySimpleConfChange`: at a follower with the full configuration, an identity
  effect. A step-down `BecomeFollower` (a candidate's lost election, bf = false) is the same case.
  Both need the premise "node `id`'s bootstrap segment is over". Nothing in `trCap`/`simNode`
  records this yet; a persistent per-node witness set at its first non-bootstrap line would do.
- **The bootstrap lines themselves** (`InitState`, `BecomeFollower`, `ApplyConfChange` at
  `newRaft`/`Bootstrap`). Here `t.next` keeps `σ` (`Trace.lean`, `Trace.next`). `LineEffect`
  assumes `bootstrap = false`, so these need their own `SimRun` extension. The site has no
  `ownRaftPhysCV` (round 1's S5, open).
- **Tokens for self-addressed messages.** The self-acknowledgement and the self vote are received
  at `Advance` and are traced (`recvEvt`), so `trCap_fire_recv` needs their `inFlight` tokens. The
  `Ready` law must hand them to the `RawNode`, which must keep them until `Advance`. This should be
  stated in the `Ready` law's design. I have not verified where `simNode` will live; the notes leave
  it open.

## Second tier

### A8. `FxPres` quantifies over every witness, so its prover can tie `a'` to `a` only through `Φ'`, which `DurS` defeats

**Principle:** a bridge stated from its consumer's goal.

**Evidence.**
- `FxPres` is `∀ a' P'` with `Φ' (toC a') ∧ LineEffect …`.
- Its prover cannot extract `a' = action(a)` from `LineEffect`. That conclusion is conditional on
  `(withNode σ i a' …).violated = .ok none`, which `simFx σ'` (the goal) is what gives. So `a'` is
  known only up to `toC` via `Φ'`.
- `toC` keeps `matchIndex` only on `srv` (`TraceSimGhost.lean:109-113`), while `DurS` bounds
  `∀ j, (σ.s i).matchIndex j` (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:81`, unrestricted `j`).
- So for any line leaving a leader (`Commit`, `Replicate`, the leader's sends), `FxPres` must
  prove `DurS` for every `a'` whose `matchIndex` outside the voters is arbitrary. That is false.

**Fix.**
- Cheap: `∀ j ∈ σ.server` in `DurS` (`DurInvFixed` already has it). Also check `SpecInv` for other
  reads that `toC` does not determine.
- Better: state the phase step in Iris, so that the witness carries its own preservation:
  `PhaseStep` yields `∃ a' P', ⌜Φ' … ∧ LineEffect …⌝ ∗ (∀ σ, … -∗ simFx σ -∗ simFx σ')`. This also
  cuts each caller's work to one proof per line.

## What is good

**The laws cover the line kinds by their check, not by their hook.** There is one law per way the
trace specification treats a line:
- a fresh line: `trCap_fire`, or `trCap_fire_booted` past the segment;
- a receive line: `trCap_fire_recv`;
- `Ready`: `trCap_fire_ready`;
- a bootstrap line: `trCap_fire_boot`;
- `BecomeFollower`: `trCap_fire_bf`.

**`bfCond` is the specification's test, and its premise matches raft.** `bfCond`
(`TracedHooksDraft.lean:213-219`) is `isBecomeFollowerInUpdateTermOrReturnToFollower`'s test
(`Trace.lean:506-521`), with "the node's last line" as `LastOf` keyed by the id string, as the
specification compares `ev.nid`. I checked every `becomeFollower` call raft makes in this
workload against it:
- `Step`'s higher-term branch (`raft.go:1137-1141`): skipped, because the receive line is
  logged just before at `:1112`;
- a candidate's same-term `MsgApp`/`MsgHeartbeat`: skipped by the second disjunct;
- `poll`'s `VoteLost`: not skipped, a candidate stepping down.

That last case is the only non-skipped one, so `PhaseStepBF`'s "leader or candidate" premise
(`:633`) is one the caller has. `bfSrv` is `BecomeFollowerOfTerm` at the server's own term
(`Spec.lean:329-331`).

**The booted flag and the last event live in `simInv`.** `simInv`
(`TraceSimGhost.lean:214-223`) holds them:

    b = true ↔ booted L i,  LastOf L (decStr i) o,  b = true → o.isSome

The laws set them from their events. This is the minimal state the skip bit and the bootstrap
classification need, and it is the simulation's own: no raft ghost.

**`trCap_fire_ready` moves the pending bag as `State.ready` does** (`Spec.lean:323-326`). The share
empties, the node's phantoms go from `phP` to `phM`, and fresh token keys come from `keyBound`.
The self-addressed messages' tokens come out too, which is all `Advance` needs: `acceptReady`
keeps them in `stepsOnAdvance` (`rawnode.go:414-418`).

**`lineEffect_staleReq` and `phantomOf` match the specification.**
- `rejectAppendEntriesRequest` answers subtype `"app"` whatever the request's subtype, with
  `success := false` when pre-vote and check-quorum are off (`Spec.lean:385-390`).
- `handleRequestVoteRequest` rejects a lower-term request (`Spec.lean:340-349`).
- `phantomOf` (`TraceSimMatch.lean:4613-4617`) is exactly these, at the receiver's own term.
- `lineEffect_staleResp` is `DropStaleResponse`.

**`lineEffect_appRecv`'s premises are the ones raft's `handleAppendEntries` establishes**
(`raft.go:1806-1818`):
- `hcom : committed ≤ m.index` is the early return's negation;
- `happ` is `maybeAppend`'s success;
- `hbe` and `hwf` come with the message's facts.

`lineEffect_staleRecv`'s `hst` is the strict early return, answered with `committed`, which is
`stResp`'s `matchIndex := a.commitIndex`.

**The ASCII clause is cheap and closed.**
- `nodeEvOf_ascii` and `sendEvOf_ascii` derive it from the event builders;
- `trCap_fire_bf` derives it itself;
- `simInv` keeps it for `ConvertEvents`.

## First tier (wrong or unusable for the closed theorem)

### B1. `trCap_fire_ready` hands out tokens for an unknown list of messages

**Principle:** "the postcondition says what the caller inspects"; complete.

**Evidence.** The post (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:581-583`) is

    ∃ (tn) (P) (Bn), traceDir γd tn ∗ ⌜(Bn's values).Perm P⌝ ∗ [∗map] k ↦ m ∈ Bn, inFlight tn k m

`P` is a fresh existential. Nothing ties it to the share's pending list, which `Φ` relates to raft's
outbox (`SimIdle`: `P = pendAbs C outbox`, `:101`).

**Caller affected.**
- The application sends `rd.Messages` (`examples/raft/tracecheck/concurrent.go:276-283`). The
  receiving node's `trCap_fire_recv` needs `inFlight tn k m` with `m` that message's `msgAbs`
  (`:550-552`).
- With this post, the `Ready` caller cannot say which physical message a token belongs to.
  No receipt's token premise can then be supplied.

**Fix.** The proof already has `HΦ : Φ C cs P` for the very `P` it returns
(`TracedHooksGlobal.lean:622,723`). Export it:

    ∃ tn P Bn, traceDir γd tn ∗ ⌜tn.voters = C₀ ∧ ∃ cs, Φ C₀ cs P⌝ ∗ ⌜(Bn's values).Perm P⌝ ∗ …

Or quantify `C` in the post and state `∃ cs, Φ C cs P` there. With `SimIdle`, the caller then gets
`P = pendAbs C outbox`.

### B2. `appMid` admits truncations the search never visits, so the accepted append's `FxPresR` is unprovable

**Principles:** complete; "a bridge is stated from its consumer's goal".

**Evidence.**
- `appMid` (`TraceSimMatch.lean:4481-4485`) admits every length `k` with `p ≤ k < log.length`
  (`p` the request's `prevLogIndex`). `lineEffect_appRecv` uses it as its `Mid` (`:4500`), and so
  does `appRecvU` (`:4724`).
- The specification removes one entry at a time from the log's end, and only while the overlap
  conflicts (`ConflictAppendEntriesRequest`, `Spec.lean:432-436`). So it visits only
  `d - 1 ≤ k < log.length`, `d` being the first conflicting index. `maybeAppend_spec` names `d`
  (`TraceSimMatch.lean:1875`, `⟨d, hd, hpd, …⟩`).
- `chain_appAccept` nevertheless asks for the invariants at every `k ≥ p` (`hinvT`, `:1859-1860`).
- `FxPresR` must give `simFx` at every `Mid` state (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:542`). At an unvisited
  `k < d - 1`, `QuorumLogF` (`:312-314`) can be false.

  Take a follower `j` in the quorum that committed the leader's index `n > k`. Truncated to `k`,
  `j` lacks the leader's committed prefix. With three servers, `j` and the third server make a
  quorum, so `isQuorum` holds and `QuorumLogF` fails.

  This happens whenever `prevLogIndex < n`: a delayed `MsgApp`, or a probe after a rejection.
  There is then no truncation at all (`d` past the overlap), but `appMid` still demands it.

**Caller affected.** `raft.handleAppendEntries`'s path, which applies `trCap_fire_recv` with
`lineEffect_appRecv`/`appRecvU`. This is the most frequent receive line.

**Fix.**
- Restate `chain_appAccept`'s `hinvT` over `d - 1 ≤ k` only.
- Restate `appMid` with the conflict bound: `∃ k, p ≤ k ∧ k < log.length ∧ ∀ q < k - p,
  m.entries[q]? = log[p + q]?` (the request's entries agree up to `k`), or `d` from
  `plogMaybeAppend`.

At the visited states, `j` already lacks every prefix it loses (its entry at `d` differs), so
`QuorumLogF` is preserved there.

### B3. `lineEffect_applyCC`'s `AccIdle` cannot be supplied for every server the share stands for

**Principles:** complete; no unneeded premise.

**Evidence.**
- `PhaseStepB` (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:602-609`) quantifies over every `a` with
  `toC (confAbs C) a = cs`.
- `toC` keeps `matchIndex` only on `C` (`TraceSimGhost.lean:110-114`).
- `AccIdle`'s last conjunct (`TraceSimMatch.lean:4401`),
  `∀ j, (nc.union lr).contains j = false → a.matchIndex j = 0`, is a condition on `matchIndex`
  outside `C`. So it is false for some `a` of every share, and `PhaseStepB` cannot be built from
  `lineEffect_applyCC`.

The specification's `ApplySimpleConfChange` zeroes those entries (`Spec.lean:315-319`). The
effect is the identity only up to `toC`.

`AccIdle`'s `a.state ≠ Etcdraft.leader` (`:4395`) is also unneeded:
- under `R`, `pendingConfChangeIndex = 0` (`TraceSim.lean:131`), so `ApplySimpleConfChange` at a
  leader takes the `else` branch (`Spec.lean:313`) and leaves the server as it is;
- raft's caller would have to prove "not a leader" at `RawNode.ApplyConfChange`. That follows only
  from `hup`'s `hasUnappliedConfChanges` refusal (`raft.go:1001,1013-1038`), and `ownRaftPhysCV`
  does not record that (I did not find it).

**Caller affected.** The application's `ApplyConfChange` of the bootstrap entries after the first
`Ready` (`concurrent.go:311-326`), through `switchToConfig`'s `traceConfChangeEvent`
(`raft.go:1981`). Every run logs these lines, three per node.

**Fix.** State the effect as `a ↦ { a with matchIndex := fun j => if (nc.union lr).contains j then
a.matchIndex j else 0 }`. Its `toC` equals `a`'s, so `Φ'` is unaffected. Then drop that conjunct.
Replace `a.state ≠ leader` by `a.pendingConfChangeIndex = 0`, which `R` gives.

### B4. Receive lines the workload produces with no effect lemma

**Principle:** complete (every line kind needs a `LineEffect` for its caller's `PhaseStepR`).

**Evidence.**
- **`MsgHeartbeatResp` at any term.**
  - `lineEffect_resp` takes `htyev : appResp ∨ voteResp` (`TraceSimMatch.lean:3924`).
  - `respLine` is the same (`:2807`).
  - `isAEResp_any` takes `hty : m.ty = .appResp` (`:2229`).
  - No line-predicate lemma exists for `.heartbeatResp` (`grep` finds it only in
    `lineEffect_skipResp`, `:3595`).

  Every leader receives one per follower per tick (`concurrent.go:162`, `HeartbeatTick: 1`).
  The specification's effect is `handleAppendEntriesResponse` with `matchIndex 0`: `respSrv`
  leaves the server as it is.
- **A lower-term `MsgHeartbeat`.** `lineEffect_staleReq` takes `m.ty = .app ∨ .vote` (`:4623`).
  `receive_staleAER` is subtype-generic (`TraceSimLemmas.lean:2810-2813`), and `isAER_heartbeat`
  exists (`TraceSimMatch.lean:411`). It is routine after each of `DefaultCConfig`'s three
  isolations: the old leader's heartbeats reach followers of a higher term.
- **A lower-term `MsgHeartbeatResp`.** `lineEffect_staleResp` takes the same `htyev` (`:4572`).
- **Second-round receipts at a higher term, as `LineEffect`s.** `simRun` versions exist
  (`simRun_recvVoteReqHigher`, `RespHigher`, `HeartbeatU`, `StaleU`, `RejectU`,
  `:2520-3021`), but the laws consume `LineEffect`s. Only `lineEffect_appRecvU` has one.
  - A higher-term `MsgVote` comes up in every election.
  - A higher-term heartbeat, stale append or rejected append comes up after every leader change.

  `lineEffect_recv2` can carry each of them, but only by re-proving its `hrcv` (the second round's
  `receive` equation and `oneLessMessage`) inside the caller.

**Fix.**
- Extend `htyev` in `lineEffect_resp`, `lineEffect_staleResp` and `lineEffect_staleReq` to the
  heartbeat types. Add `isAEResp_heartbeatResp` beside `isAEResp_any`.
- Add the `LineEffect` forms of the five `U`/`Higher` steps, as `appRecvU` is to `simRun_recvAppU`.
  Alternatively, add one composition lemma, `FirstRound` plus a one-round effect lemma at `u`,
  with `Mid := (· = u) ∪ Mid'`.

### B5. The bootstrap lines' hooks have no traced statement, and the capability's configuration clause is false there

**Principles:** complete; "a clause is stated over what its user holds".

**Evidence.**
- **No traced statements.** There is no `traceInitState.tracedSpec` and no
  `traceConfChangeEvent.tracedSpec`. Their current specs take only `⌜TraceLoggerOk tl⌝` and post
  `True` (`Spec/go_etcd_io/raft/v3/traceInitState.lean`, `traceConfChangeEvent.lean`). Both are
  logged in every traced run:
  - `newRaft` logs `InitState` (`raft.go:469`) and an `ApplyConfChange` through `switchToConfig`;
  - `RawNode.Bootstrap` logs one `ApplyConfChange` per peer (`bootstrap.go:77-79`);
  - the application logs the post-segment `ApplyConfChange` (B3).
- **No event predicate for `ApplyConfChange`.** `NodeEvOf` forces `e.propCC = none`
  (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:89`), while `traceConfChangeEvent` writes `prop["cc"]` with `newconf`
  (`state_trace.go:268-283`).
- **The configuration clause is false during bootstrap.** `trCapB` requires
  `(elements c.incoming).Perm C ∧ id ∈ C` (`:384`). At `newRaft`, raft's configuration is empty;
  inside `Bootstrap` it is `{1}`, then `{1,2}`. A bootstrap hook's statement written like the
  others (`ownRaftPhysCV c … ∗ trCap … c …`, with `c` raft's) has an unsatisfiable capability.
  `trCap_fire_boot` does not use `c`, so the caller can pass another `c`. But then a law to re-key
  the capability to raft's `c` once bootstrap is over is needed, and none exists. `newRaft` has no
  `ownRaftPhysCV` either (round 1's S5, still open).

**Fix.**
- Add `CCEvOf id s c cfg e`: `NodeEvOf` with `e.name = "ApplyConfChange"` and
  `e.propCC = some ⟨[], (Slice of cfg.incoming).map decStr⟩`. Add its ASCII lemma.
- Add `traceConfChangeEvent.tracedSpec` and `traceInitState.tracedSpec`.
- State `trCap_fire_boot` over a capability without the configuration clause, or add
  `trCapB_cfg : (elements c'.incoming).Perm C → c'.outgoing = ∅ → id ∈ C → trCapB … c … ⊢ trCapB … c' …`.

### B6. `simFx` gives no source for two hypotheses its receipts' `FxPresR` need

**Principle:** "every premise has a source the client sees".

**Evidence.**
- **(a) `QuorumLogF` at a follower's raised commit.** `maybeAppend` and `handleHeartbeat` raise a
  follower's commit (`raft.go:1815,1851`); in the specification, `AppendEntriesAlreadyDone`'s
  `commitTo` does (`Spec.lean:422-426`). Then `FxPresR` must re-establish `QuorumLogF` at the
  follower's longer committed prefix.

  It follows from `QuorumLogF` at the sender if the request's commit is within its sender's
  `commitIndex`. Neither `SpecInv` nor `msgFacts` says so: `ReqCommit` bounds it by the leader's
  log length only (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:111-113`).

  So every heartbeat receipt that commits, and every accepted or stale append, is blocked as
  `maybeCommit` is. The Draft names only `maybeCommit`. Unlike `maybeCommit`, this one needs no
  acknowledgement history.
- **(b) `CommitInSenderDurable`.** `specInv_appendEntriesAlreadyDone` takes it
  (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:1446-1449,1476`): the follower's commit is within the leader's *durable*
  log. `durable` is the simulation's: `SimServerR` excludes it (`TraceSim.lean:124-126`), and
  `srvFacts` does not mention it (`TraceSimFacts.lean:255-263`). Raft's ghost therefore cannot
  give it.

  `SpecInv` has `DurS` for the leader's own commit and match indices (`:77-90`), but nothing for a
  follower's commit. The header says it comes from "leader completeness, and the candidate's
  persisting" (`:28-30`). The second half is a fact about `durable` that `CandS` keeps only while
  the server is a candidate.

  **Uncertain:** I found no derivation from `simFx`. If there is one, the docstring should name it.

**Callers affected.** Every follower receipt of `MsgApp`/`MsgHeartbeat` that takes
`AppendEntriesAlreadyDone` (`lineEffect_appRecv`, `staleRecv`, `heartbeatRecv`, the `U`
variants), through `FxPresR`.

**Fix.** Add two pure `SpecInv` clauses, preserved by the actions (pure facts over the state, as
the principles prefer):
- `ReqCommitC`: an append request in either bag has `commitIndex ≤ (σ.s source).commitIndex`.
  Commit indices only grow, and the request carries its sender's commit at the time.
- `LeaderDurPrefix`: a leader's durable log contains every entry of a term below its own. Its log
  at `BecomeLeader` is persisted, by `CandS`, and only grows after.

With leader completeness (`votesTbl`) and `ReqCommit` strengthened to the durable log for requests
in `messages`, these give `CommitInSenderDurable`. Then state in `maybeCommit`'s docstring and the
plan that only the leader's `Commit` waits on the history copy.

## Second tier

### C1. `FxPresReady` and `FxPresBF` are closed lemmas, not premises

**Principle:** no unneeded premise (caller burden).

**Evidence.**
- `State.ready` changes only the server's `durable` and moves messages between the bags. `srvFacts`
  reads no `durable`. `specInv_ready` is unconditional (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:834`). `msgFacts`
  and `MsgsUniq` are over the union of the bags, which `ready` permutes.
- The step-down keeps logs, commit and term. `specInv_stepDownToFollower` needs only `i ∈ σ.server`
  (`:530`), and `srvFacts`' leader clause becomes `True`.

Every `Ready` or `BecomeFollower` caller would re-prove the same lemma.

**Fix.** Prove `fxPresReady γ id E Φ` and `fxPresBF γ id Φ` once, in the laws' file, and drop them
from `trCap_fire_ready`'s and `trCap_fire_bf`'s premises.

### C2. The hook specs' posts forget the booted flag and the last event, which the laws keep

**Principle:** "know the margin" (the post is weaker than what the laws give).

**Evidence.**
- Every non-bootstrap law posts `trCapB … true Φ' Λ'` (`:514,555,583,617,654`).
- `raft.send.tracedSpec`, `traceCommit.tracedSpec` and `raft.maybeCommit.tracedSpec` post
  `trCap … Φ'` (`:697,715,812`), which drops `b` and puts `Λ := True`.
- `trCap_fire_booted` and `trCap_fire_bf` take `trCapB … true` (`:618,654`), and the BF law reads
  `Λ`. The `RawNode` keeps the capability from `Ready` to the application's `ApplyConfChange`,
  which needs `trCapB … true` (B3's path). Through any spec posting `trCap`, the flag is lost.

**Fix.**
- Take `trCapB … b Φ Λ` in the pre with `b` and `Λ` quantified.
- In the post, give `trCapB … true Φ' Λ'` when a line is logged, and `b`, `Λ` unchanged when none is.

### C3. `trCap_fire` and `trCap_fire_booted` overlap

`PhaseStepB` is `PhaseStep` without the bootstrap-name condition. The two laws differ only in
whether `b` is known.

**Fix.** Keep one law over `trCapB … b`, with `PhaseStepB` and the premise
`b = false → ∀ e, E e → isBootstrapName (gsStr e.name) = false`. A caller then never chooses
between two laws.

### C6. `MsgsUniq` over every `Line` (A11, open)

Most of it is structural. For vote requests, vote responses, append responses and heartbeats, the
abstract message (`msgAbs`, `TraceSim.lean:92-119`) has exactly the fields its `Line.is*` compares.
Two messages that one line matches are then equal, with no raft fact needed. Only `MsgApp` (the
entries' contents, which `isAppendEntriesRequest` does not compare) needs `msgFacts`.

**Fix.** State `MsgsDet` over the four predicates as a lemma for the structural types, and keep a
`simFx` clause only for append requests with entries. Every `FxPres(R)` prover's obligation then
shrinks to that case.

`FormatUint(m.GetVote(), 10)` (`state_trace.go:155`). This is harmless. Putting
`tm.vote = decStr m.vote` in `MsgEvOf` would be uniform, if `Msg` has the field.

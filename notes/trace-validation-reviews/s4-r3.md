## First tier

### A1. The generic logger contract is unprovable for `SyncRecorder`, and gives `Main` nothing to read

**Claim** (note, "Increment 2"):

    isTraceLogger γt tl := ⌜tl = nil⌝ ∨ ∀ ev Q, AU⟨∀ L, traceAuth γt L⟩⟨traceAuth γt (L ++ [ev]), Q⟩ -∗ WP tl.TraceEvent(ev) {Q}

"The harness's `SyncRecorder` proves it with `traceAuth γt L` in its lock invariant."

**Evidence.**
- **The argument is a pointer.** `TraceLogger.TraceEvent(*TracingEvent)` (`state_trace.go`, `type TraceLogger`). Raft allocates a fresh `&TracingEvent{...}` per line (`traceEvent`), with:
  - a `*TracingMessage`;
  - two fresh `[]string` configuration slices;
  - a `map[string]any` holding a `*TracingConfChange` (or `uint64`s for `match`/`next`).
- **The recorder dereferences it.** `SyncRecorder.TraceEvent` does `r.events = append(r.events, *ev)` (`concurrent.go:63-66`), a load through `ev`. Under the contract as written, it holds no points-to for `ev`, so its WP cannot be proved.
- **The copy is shallow, and `Main` reads through it.** `ConvertEvents` (`main.go:43-74`) reads `ev.Message` and `ev.Conf[0/1]`, and type-asserts `ev.Properties["cc"].(*raft.TracingConfChange)` and its slices. All of these are raft's allocations, reached through the copied struct.
- **`L` holds pointers.** As written, `L` is a list of Go pointers. The law "`simInv γt ∗ traceAuth γt L` ⊢ `L` steps to a unique state" needs `L`'s *events*, and `Main` needs the Go values it reads to be those events.

**Who it affects.**
- The harness (layer D): the recorder's proof.
- `Main`'s proof: from the recorder's events to `traceAccepts`.
- Raft's hooks: what they must hand over.

**Repair.**
- Index the trace by abstract events: `L : List Event`, the checker's `Event` that `ConvertEvents` produces, or a raft-side record with a pure map to it.
- Make the contract hand over a persistent, read-only representation of the pointed-to event:

      isTraceLogger γt tl := ⌜tl = nil⌝ ∨
        □ ∀ (p : Loc) (e : Event) Q, tracingEventRep p e -∗
            AU⟨∀ L, traceAuth γt L⟩⟨traceAuth γt (L ++ [e]), Q⟩ -∗ WP tl.TraceEvent(p) {Q}

  - `tracingEventRep p e` is `↦□` of the struct, its message, its slices, its map and the map's values, with `e` their abstract value.
  - Raft can give `↦□` because it never touches the event after the call.
  - Read-only shares fit the shallow copy.
  - Say that `traceEvent`'s non-nil spec builds `tracingEventRep` and supplies `e = eventOf …`.
- Also mark the persistence (`□`), which the note's text leaves implicit; see C4.

### A2. `MoreUpToDateCorrectInv` is not one of raft's safety theorems: a witness against raft with five voters and size-limited batches

**Claim** (note, "The invariants"): "`LogInv`, `QuorumLogInv`, `MoreUpToDateCorrectInv`, ... are raft's safety theorems: route (a) from `isCommitted_agree`, `lc_core`, `cq_agree`, the leader's log records."

**Evidence.** Under the specification's own trace semantics, raft violates it with no stale leader and no specification gap.

- **Witness:** `/tmp/claude-1000/-workspace-etcd-grove/8e48033c-792a-435a-bae3-16d75b0d1a02/scratchpad/r3/tc/witness_mutd_test.go`.
  - It uses a copy of the module whose `sim.go` takes `MaxSizePerMsg` from a variable, set to 1 in the test.
  - It has five voters. The bootstrap entries are 1-5.
- **The schedule:**
  1. Node 1 leads term 2. Its entry x at 6 (term 2) reaches node 3 only.
  2. Node 2 leads term 3 (votes of 4 and 5). Its entry y at 6 (term 3) reaches node 4. Node 1 steps down.
  3. Node 1 leads term 4 (votes of 3 and 5) and appends z at 7.
  4. Node 5 is probed down and receives x alone (a cut batch). It acks 6.
  5. z reaches nodes 3 and 4, so 7 is committed at term 4, and x with it.
  6. A heartbeat to node 5 carries `min(Match 6, 7) = 6`.
- **The violation.**
  - Node 5's committed prefix ends at x (term 2), and node 5's last term is 2.
  - Node 2, a follower of term 4, has last term 3.
  - So `IsPrefix(Committed(5), log[2])` fails.
  - No leader is stale: node 2 stepped down at node 1's term-4 `MsgVote`, so this is not item 7.

  ```
  cd .../scratchpad/r3/tc; PATH=/usr/local/go/bin:$PATH GOTOOLCHAIN=go1.27.1 GOFLAGS=-mod=mod \
    go test -tags with_tla -count=1 -run TestWitnessMoreUpToDate -v .      # ok (asserts the violation)
  go run ./cmd/checktrace -explain ../mutd.ndjson
  ../mutd.ndjson: 251 events: direct: invariant MoreUpToDateCorrectInv violated at line 249; porcupine: Illegal
  ```

  Line 249 is node 5's `ReceiveAppendEntriesRequest` (`MsgHeartbeat`, commit 6, its log 6). Every earlier line matched.
- **Not cross-checked with TLC.** No TLA+ tools were available here. The Go transcription agreed with TLC on every comparison so far, so I expect TLC agrees. This is uncertain until run.

**Why `Main` does not reach it, and what the proof must use instead.**
- The invariant needs, for every server `j`: `lastTerm(log[j]) ≥` the term in which `j` learned its commit index. Leader completeness then covers the rest.
- That fact holds only because every `MsgApp` carries the leader's log to its last index, which includes the leader's empty entry of its term. So a follower that learns a commit index in term T has an entry of term T.
- This is true here because of:
  - P5's unlimited `MaxSizePerMsg` and unthrottled inflights (`maybeSendAppend` sends `[Next, lastIndex]`);
  - or, separately, three voters: a commit quorum for z is the leader plus one node, which then holds z.
- P5 as stated ("each carries `Commit ≤ Index + len(Entries)`") is weaker than what is needed. `lc_core`, `cq_agree` and `isCommitted_agree` do not give it.

**Who it affects.**
- Layer B's plan for the invariants, which is "workload-agnostic" by the plan's decision ("Not tied to the workload"): this invariant needs a premise on raft's configuration.
- The closed theorem: not false, since `DefaultCConfig` has three voters and unlimited batches.
- `notes/raft-trace-validation.md`: an eighth place where `etcdraft.tla` does not fit raft. The upstream invariant fails with ≥ 5 voters and size-limited batches, raft's default configuration (1 MiB) with large entries.

**Repair.**
- Strengthen P5 to the fact used: "every `MsgApp` carries the leader's log from `Next` to its last index."
- Add the per-server clause: "`σ.s j`'s commit index was learned from a leader of a term ≤ `lastTerm(σ.s j.log)`". It is maintained at receive lines from that P5 fact. Alternatively, state it as a σ-level message invariant (route (b)): every append request in the bags ends at its sender's last index at send time.
- Move `MoreUpToDateCorrectInv` out of "raft's safety theorems".
- Record item 8 with the witness. Upstream reporting is the user's decision, as for item 7.

## Second tier

### B1. `ElectionSafetyInv` is routed through votes, but it is about logs, and the table has no fact that gives it

**Claim.**
- "`MoreThanOneLeaderInv`, `ElectionSafetyInv` need an invariant over every record of a granted vote ... `VotesOk` ... plus `isLeader_unique`."
- Table row 1 lists `ElectionSafetyInv` under "`σ.s j` a leader of T". Row 4 lists it under `isVote`.

**Evidence.**
- `electionSafetyInv` (`Invariants.lean`) states: for a leader `i`, `maxIndexOfTerm(log_i, currentTerm_i) ≥ maxIndexOfTerm(log_j, currentTerm_i)` for every `j`. That is, the leader's log holds the highest-indexed entry of its own term.
- The fact it needs is "while `i` leads T, every entry of term T anywhere is at an index ≤ `len(σ.s i.log)`".
  - This is an *upper bound* on the term-T records.
  - The persistent facts in the table (`isEntryR`, `logRecs`, `isLeader`) cannot express it.
  - Raft holds it as the term log's authoritative part, owned by node `i`. Another node's hook (a follower appending i's entries) cannot see that part.
- **`MoreThanOneLeaderInv` does not need `VotesOk`.** Two σ-leaders of one term are both `isLeader` by the table's row 1, and `isLeader_unique` (`Ghost.lean:1111`) concludes. This is uncertain only if raft records the win after `traceBecomeLeader` rather than at `poll`'s `win` rule; S7 says it is recorded at the `win` rule.

**Repair.**
- Route `ElectionSafetyInv` by (b): a σ invariant "every append request of term T in the bags carries a slice of the current log of T's leader while it leads". It holds because a leader's log only grows during its term, and the specification's `ClientRequest` is the only change.
- Or give simInv a ghost upper bound updated at `Replicate` lines.
- Drop `VotesOk` unless a specific invariant needs it, and name that invariant.

### B2. The table lacks the clauses that the corrected `LeaderCompletenessInv` and `QuorumLogInv` need

- **Corrected LC** (guard `currentTerm[l] ≥ currentTerm[i]`). The argument the plan gives (Q4) needs, for each server `i`: "`σ.s i`'s committed prefix was committed (a `cq`) at a term ≤ `σ.s i.currentTerm`".
  - `isCommitted` is a lower bound on one global committed log (`monoListLbOwn γ.reCommit`, `Ghost.lean:849`) and carries no term.
  - Candidate source: `cmtLb γ ldr t n` ("the leader of t had commit ≥ n", `Ghost.lean:~1095`), received with the message that raised `i`'s commit, plus `t ≤ currentTerm[i]`. I did not check that raft's proof hands it to the receiver; this is uncertain.
- **`QuorumLogInv`** needs, for the members of a commit quorum `cq γ t n`: their *current* σ logs still extend the prefix.
  - The argument goes through `lc_core`: their term is ≥ t, so only leaders of terms ≥ t can truncate them, and those leaders hold the prefix.
  - This needs a per-server lower bound "`σ.s j.currentTerm ≥` every term `j` acked in". The table has nothing for terms.
- **Repair:** add both clauses to the table, each with its raft source, or mark it as route (b).

### B3. Bootstrap phase: one more premise, and the transition needs stating

**Claim.** "`σ` is allocated after the last `StartNode` from the recorded prefix, with an invariant that only the bootstrap's ids ever log."

**Evidence.**
- **A missing premise.** `bootstrapOf` (`Trace.lean:191-199`) ends node `i`'s bootstrap segment at its first line whose name is not `InitState`/`BecomeFollower`/`ApplyConfChange`. So `t.init` (`serverOf`'s last bootstrap line) depends on node `i`'s *later* lines too. If node `i`'s first line after `StartNode` were one of those three, `newTrace` would count it as bootstrap and change `TraceInit`.
  - In `Main` this does not happen. A node's first post-`StartNode` line is its first `Ready` or a `Receive*`:
    - `hup` refuses while `hasUnappliedConfChanges` (`raft.go:1001`);
    - the application calls `ApplyConfChange` only from a `Ready`, which is logged first, since `confc` is served after `acceptReady`;
    - `becomeFollower` only follows a receive line.
  - But it is a premise on the application (raft's API allows `ApplyConfChange` at any time), so it belongs in P4. Example wording: "no node's first line after its `StartNode` is a bootstrap-named line".
- **The transition.**
  - At the last `StartNode`, the nodes' run goroutines already own their `RawNode`s (`StartNode` forks `n.run()`, `node.go:271-275`), so the harness cannot touch their `simNode`s.
  - The design should say:
    - node `i`'s `simNode` is created at the end of its own `Bootstrap`, with the value `serverOf` will give. That value depends only on its last bootstrap line and its own `newconf`, and `matchIndex` is all-zero, so node `i` can compute it locally;
    - the harness-side transition law flips simInv from bootstrap to simulation mode using only the authoritative side and each node's persistent "bootstrapped" witness.
  - List that law with the allocation law for `γt` and simInv (C4).

### B4. `Node.Advance`'s stopped branch and the tokens

- `Node.Advance` returns without the rendezvous when `done` is closed (`node.go:555-560`). Its spec's post has a `nodeStopped` disjunct (`node__Advance.lean`).
- The note routes the tokens through `Advance`'s post, so on that branch the application has no tokens for `rd.Messages`, and the harness still sends them (`concurrent.go:276-283`).
- In `Main` the branch is unreachable: `RunConcurrent` stops nodes only after `c.apps.Wait()`. But the harness's proof needs either:
  - a law excluding `nodeStopped` while it holds a "not yet stopped" share that `Stop` consumes;
  - or the tokens on both branches. The run loop exits only from its `select`, after `acceptReady` has finished, so it can deposit them where the stopped branch reads them.
- Also, `Advance`'s post must say *which* tokens it returns (those of the `Ready`'s traced messages to others). `nodeDriver`'s `handling` names `(es, L)`, not the messages, so the driver state needs `rd.Messages`.

### B5. "Each hook's call site supplies the update" is not local

- **What the update needs.** It needs `a = σ.s i` and `R_i(s_at_hook, a)`, or the handling phase's version of it, with `s` raft's state at the hook.
- **Where the hooks sit.** Of the hooks' twelve call sites, most are in internal functions with many callers. `send` alone (`raft.go:593/599`) is called from:
  - `sendAppend`, `maybeSendAppend`, `sendHeartbeat`, `bcastAppend`, `bcastHeartbeat`;
  - `campaign`, `appendEntry`;
  - `Step`'s vote branch, `handleAppendEntries`, `handleHeartbeat`;
  - `stepLeader`'s branches.

  `becomeFollower`, `appendEntry` and `maybeCommit` are similar.
- **Specs between the API entry points and the hooks must change.** Each of those functions' specs would carry `simNode` and the phase, with its pending lines, from the entry point (`Step`, `Tick`, `Propose`, `acceptReady`, `Advance`, the application's `ApplyConfChange`) down to the hook. `R_i` does not hold between hooks: for example, `becomeLeader` after `reset` but before `traceBecomeLeader` (`raft.go:957-978`). So this cannot hide inside `ownRaftPhys` without a phase index that those functions change. This is the Local principle's cost, and revision 3's "Open" names only `raft__Step`'s 36 branches.
- **Reads at the opened sites.** The non-nil hooks read `hardState()`, `raftLog.lastIndex()` and `trk.Voters[0/1].Slice()` (`traceEvent`). At the opened sites these are not in hand:
  - at `acceptReady` the tracker is inside `Hclose`;
  - at `send_tail` the rest is in the wand `Hback`;
  - at `switchToConfig` the tracker field is mid-replacement (`traceConfChangeEvent` reads the old `r.trk.Voters`);
  - at `newRaft` the struct is a literal.

  `notes/trace-validation-hooks.md` left this open "for S4", and revision 3 does not settle it.
- **Repair.**
  - List the functions whose specs gain the phase (the call graph from each entry point to each hook).
  - State the phase predicate's laws.
  - Make "a hook and its phase through `send`'s callers" the Probe's easy proof, not the hook alone.

### B6. Coupling: `inFlight γt (msgAbs m)` puts the transcription's types into raft's public node API

- Keying the tokens by `msgAbs m` makes `Node.Step`'s spec (and `Advance`'s post) name `Msg` and `msgAbs` from `.../examples/raft/tracecheck/etcdraft/Tla/`.
- Raft's package (simInv) would then import an example's proof directory. That is a layout problem, and a coupling cost on every client of `Node.Step` even when gated.
- **Repair:** key the tokens by raft's own message abstraction: the value raft's protocol model already uses (`Msg`, `isMessage`), or the Go message's abstract value. Apply `msgAbs` inside simInv. Or move the transcription to a neutral module that raft's API may import, listed in `etc/api/raft.txt`.

### B7. P9's restatement and the FIFO harness (comment on Q5(a))

- **The argument for (a) is right with three voters.**
  - The leader's `MsgVote` to F is handed out at its campaign `Ready`, before any term-T heartbeat or `MsgApp` exists. Those need a vote back, which is sent only after the campaign `Ready`'s `Advance`.
  - On a FIFO link, F therefore gets the `MsgVote` first, or never if it is dropped.
  - With three voters, no other candidate of T can reach F: the other voter voted for the leader, so it cannot be a candidate of T.
- **But P9's wording is wrong.** It reads "a voter learns of its term's leader only after the leader's `MsgVote`, or not at all". When the `MsgVote` is dropped, the voter learns of the leader *without* it.
  - Suggested wording: "a voter that learns of its term-T leader without having voted in T never receives a `MsgVote` of term T".
- **The harness must keep the hand-out order in the enqueue.** Today `send` forks one goroutine per message (`concurrent.go:351-353`). The FIFO harness must append to the link's queue synchronously in the application goroutine, in `rd.Messages` order across successive `Ready`s. Only the per-link goroutine delivers. Otherwise two enqueues race.

## Third tier

- **C1. Argument orders in the table.** `isLeader γ id term` (`Ghost.lean:1081`) and `isVote γ v term cand` (`:815`); the table writes `isLeader γ T j` and `isVote γ T j c`. Also, the "raft fact" for `durable` is not a raft fact: it is σ's own value, and route (b) handles it.
- **C2. `Step` table.**
  - "`SendAppendEntriesRequest`×n" after `appendEntry`/`bcastAppend` should be "one per peer not paused" (`maybeSendAppend` returns early on `IsPaused`).
  - `MsgProp` at a candidate, or at a follower without a leader, is dropped with no line; add the row.
  - The `Advance` row should say the self-receipts' handling can log `Commit` and `SendAppendEntriesRequest` lines (the `MsgAppResp` row applies).
- **C3. Line numbers.** `becomeLeader`'s `reset` call is `raft.go:957`; `reset` is defined at `:799`. The note cites `raft.go:812`.
- **C4. The allocation law.**
  - The note should name the law that allocates `γt` with `traceAuth`/`traceFrag` and simInv in bootstrap mode, which the harness applies before the first `StartNode`.
  - It should name `traceFrag`'s owner: simInv, not a node.
  - The contract needs `□`.
- **C5. The handling phase's facts.** At a receive line σ already holds the appended log and the new commit index, while raft does not. The per-server clause for the receiver must therefore come from the message's `isMessage` content (the carried entries' records, the commit's certificate) combined with the receiver's records. Say so next to "a strict prefix of one".
- **C6. The corrected LC guard (comment on Q4(b)).** The guard is right as far as I can check:
  - a committed entry that `i` knows was committed at a term `tc ≤ currentTerm[i] ≤ currentTerm[l]`;
  - if `currentTerm[l] > tc`, `lc_core` applies to `l`'s log, which extends its campaign log;
  - if equal, `l` is the committer, whose log only grows in its term;
  - at a receive line's truncation states the truncated node is no longer a leader.

  It needs B2's clause.

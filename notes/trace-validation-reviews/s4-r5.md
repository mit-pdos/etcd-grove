## First tier

### A1. P9 never reaches raft's proof: as designed, the tokens make `Node.Step` unprovable at a stale `MsgVote`

**Claim.** The note (increment 2, "The `inFlight` tokens", "The tokens' key") says: an exclusive `inFlight γt m` per handed-out traced message, keyed by raft's `Msg` as a multiset; `Node.Step` takes it. P9 is stated as a FIFO-harness property, "discharged by layer D" (Premises section header).

**Evidence.**
- **The simulation step of a receipt is proved inside raft.** The `traceReceiveMessage` hook at `raft.go:1112` supplies the update, and the simulation is raft's own `simInv`.
- **Gap 6 with three voters is excluded only by P9.** The case:
  - voter `i` adopted term `T` and `lead = c` from `c`'s heartbeat, with `Vote = None`;
  - `c`'s delayed `MsgVote(T)` then arrives;
  - raft refuses: `canVote` needs `r.lead == None` (`raft.go` `Step`'s `MsgVote` case);
  - the specification grants when `logOk` holds (`Spec.lean:304-313`: `votedFor = nil`).

  At `i`'s next state-checked line, `stateMatches` compares `votedFor` (`Trace.lean:309-314`), so the simulation is lost.
- **Nothing in the token says the `MsgVote` is out of order.** A token is "a message value, owned by whoever holds it". A client holding the token of `c`'s `MsgVote(T)` may call `Node.Step` with it after the heartbeat has been delivered. Under the stated contract the step is legal, and its simulation obligation is false.
- **Layer D cannot discharge P9 at the end either.** By the end, raft's hook has already had to produce `σ'` related by `R`.

**Who it affects.**
- `Node.Step`/`RawNode.Step`'s increment-2 spec (unprovable as listed).
- Q6 (the user's decision is about an incomplete list).
- The FIFO harness of layer D.

**Repair.** Make the order part of the token. The note must state this and add it to Q6.
- Give each token its position on its link `(src, dst)`, assigned at the `Ready` line in `rd.Messages` order.
- `simInv` keeps a per-link frontier: the highest position received.
- `Node.Step` takes a token whose position is above the frontier. The FIFO harness proves this from a per-link ghost queue its delivery goroutine owns.
- `simInv` adds the clause: "if `σ.s i`'s raft `lead = c` in term `T` and `i` has not voted in `T`, then the frontier of `c→i` is past `c`'s `MsgVote(T)` to `i`". `c`'s `MsgVote(T)` is handed out at its campaign `Ready`, before any heartbeat of `T` (P8, three voters). The frontier is moved at the receipt that set `lead`.

The alternative is Q5's option (b) (the vote condition in the transcription). If it were taken, the order would not be needed.

## Second tier

### B1. `QuorumLogInv` is not one of raft's theorems, and the table's clauses do not give it

**Claim.** "`LogInv`, `QuorumLogInv`, `LeaderCompletenessInv`, `CommittedIsDurableInv` ... are raft's safety theorems: route (a) from `isCommitted_agree`, `lc_core`, `cq_agree`, the leader's log records". The table's sources for it are `logRecs`, `isCommitted` and the term row.

**Evidence.**
- **What the invariant needs.** `quorumLogInv` (`Invariants.lean:56-62`) is about the *current* logs of a quorum: a majority of the configuration has a log extending `Committed(i)`.
  - Raft proves nothing about current logs of a quorum (`grep -rn QuorumLog Proof/go_etcd_io/raft/` finds nothing).
  - At a `Commit` line, the new committed prefix's quorum is the `cq`'s `q`. Each member `v`'s *current* `σ` log must hold `G[1..n]`.
- **Why the listed facts fail.**
  - Take a member `v` that acked `(t, i ≥ n)` with `commit_v < n`, then moved to term `t+1` on a `MsgVote` without a log change.
  - Raft's `acksOk` keeps `ackAgree` only for acknowledgements of the current term above the commit (`GhostState.lean:455-470`).
  - So `logRecs`, `isCommitted` and `commitIn` of `v` say nothing about `v`'s log length or term at `n`.
  - The specification's invariant still holds there; only the route is missing.
- **What raft does have.** Raft keeps per node, for *every* acknowledgement, the history of its current full log: `histsOk γ s kl F acks := [∗map] k ∈ acks, hist γ F k.1 k.2 (bnd s kl)` (`GhostState.lean:554-556`, held as `#Hhists` at `:2733`). `hist` (`Ghost.lean:3509`) says `F` agrees with an `okL` log at each index up to `i`, or two `okL` logs diverge below. With a `cq` at `(t, n)`, every `okL` log agrees with `G` up to `n` (`lc_core`/`cq_agree`), so `F ⊇ G[1..n]`. This is what raft's own Leader Completeness uses for voters (`lc_adj_gen`, `LeaderCompleteness.lean:176-189`).

**Who it affects.** S5, which proves `QuorumLogInv` at every `Commit` line and at every follower commit raise.

**Repair.**
- Reclassify `QuorumLogInv` as "not raft's".
- Add a per-server clause: `simInv` holds `histsOk` of `σ.s j`'s log for `j`'s acknowledgements.
  - It is refreshed at `j`'s hooks. Every acknowledgement leaves through a `MsgAppResp` send, whose hook fires before the leader can receive it.
  - It is tied to `isAcked` through the bag's responses (each successful response in the bags is in its source's set).
- Note that `hist` must be shown at truncation states. A removed index `j` takes the divergence disjunct at the first conflict `p ≤ j`, between the receiver's old `okL` log and the sender's `leadFull`. It needs `bnd` above the sender's term: to check.

### B2. The commit-point clause's maintenance needs simulation-level clauses the note does not list

**Claim.** The source is `commitIn` "which `isMessage` carries ... strengthened to `t ≤ term(log[c])` by P5 (a `MsgApp` carries the leader's `committed`, a heartbeat `min(Match, committed)` with `Match` 0, past the leader's empty entry, or the receiver's own commit)".

**Evidence.**
- `isMessage` carries `commitIn γ F m.commit m.term`, whose `commitJ` gives only `t ≤ m.term` (`GhostState.lean:198`, `:216`; `Ghost.lean:3248-3252`).
- The strengthening is a fact about the *sender's state*: for a `MsgApp`, the leader's own commit-point clause at send time.
- For a heartbeat with `c > commit_j`, the case analysis needs the leader's `Match[j] ∈ {0} ∪ [E_T, ∞) ∪ {≤ a past commit of j}`, where `E_T` is the empty entry's index.
  - That needs every successful term-`T` response's index to be `≥ E_T` (P5) or the responder's commit (the `prev < committed` reply, `Spec.lean:355`).
  - Neither raft's `isMessage .heartbeat` (only `∃ i ≥ commit, isAcked dst term i`) nor the per-server table has this.
- Without it, the receiver cannot derive `t ≤ term(log_j[c])` in the case `term(Fl[c]) < T`.

**Who it affects.** S5, at every append-request and heartbeat receive line, for `MoreUpToDateCorrectInv`.

**Repair.** Add to `simInv`:
- per append request or heartbeat in the bags: the sender's strengthened certificate for its `commitIndex`;
- per leader `l` of `T`: `σ.matchIndex[l][j]` is `0`, `≥ E_T`, or `≤ σ.s j.commitIndex`;
- per successful term-`T` response in the bags: the same range for its `matchIndex`.

These are route-(b) clauses with P5 at each send.

### B3. The `int` bounds: the log-length formula does not give `< 2^63`, and the term bound's source is not the proposal budget

**Claim.** "Terms below `2^62` from a term ceiling, the log length from the boot entries and terms × (proposals + 1), both from the budget".

**Evidence.**
- **The arithmetic.** With terms `< 2^62`, `terms × 61` reaches `61 · 2^62 > 2^63`, so the stated bound does not give the `int` conversion's `< 2^63`.
- **The tighter count.**
  - Each entry beyond the boot entries is created by one leader's `appendEntry`.
  - Each term adds at most one empty entry.
  - Each proposal's entry is appended at most once: the budget credit, or raft's ticket, D21 M6.
  - So a log has at most `|boot| + terms + B` entries.
- **The term ceiling.** It is not from the proposal budget. Raft's term budget (`budgetInv`, `InvNodeChan.lean:183-187`) bounds terms by time receipts with `receiptBound GF ≤ 2^64 - 2`. That is too weak for `int`.
  - A `2^62` bound needs the adequacy theorem's `receiptBound` lower.
  - Per the bounds discipline (`PRINCIPLES.md`, "A bound is reasoned about where it is owned"), it is stated where the receipts' bound is owned: a premise of the allocation law or of the adequacy theorem, not a raft spec's `receiptBound`.

**Who it affects.** S5 (each hook's `e = eventOf …`) and S7 (adequacy).

**Repair.**
- State the log bound as `|boot| + terms + B`.
- Name the term bound's source (raft's `termLB`/`budgetInv`, with `receiptBound < 2^62` chosen by the adequacy theorem) and where it enters.

### B4. The proposal budget's route through the network is inconsistent; raft's tickets may already carry it

**Claim.**
- P5: "one taken by each `Node.Propose`/`RawNode.Propose` ... and carried by a forwarded `MsgProp`".
- Increment 2 and Q6: `Advance` gives "the tokens of the `Ready`'s traced messages", `Step` takes "a traced message's token, and `Propose` a budget credit".

**Evidence.**
- **The inconsistency.** A forwarded `MsgProp` is untraced. Under the list as written, its credit has no route from the follower's `Ready` to the leader's `Step`.
- **What raft already has.** Exclusive per-proposal tickets that ride exactly that route:
  - `entTk`/`msgTks` (`Ghost.lean:1351-1359`, "a proposal's at most once");
  - `stTks` in `msgCredits`, which `node.Step`'s `stepIn` takes (`ApiVocab.lean:471-473`, `InvApi.lean:2085-2089`).
- **Consequence.** If a ticket is consumed by the append that fires its proposal, then "entries appended ≤ tickets minted ≤ credits taken at `Propose`". The budget is then needed only at `Propose`. This follows "reuse the state the library already keeps" (`PRINCIPLES.md`, "A pure fact over the state before new ghost state"). Whether the ticket is consumed at the append I did not verify: uncertain.

**Repair.** Choose and state one route:
- (a) credits only at `Propose`, with the per-term bound from the tickets; or
- (b) credits carried by `MsgProp`, which adds them to `Advance`'s post and `Step`'s pre.

In either case, add the result to Q6.

### B5. The term row is not maintainable as a `simInv` clause, and does not need to be one

**Claim.** The table row says "`σ.s j.currentTerm` at least every term `j` acknowledged in": `j`'s term records, for every server `j`, in `simInv`.

**Evidence.**
- **The source is node-local.** It is raft's `AcksTerm s acks` (`GhostState.lean:452-454`), over `j`'s own ack map, whose authority is `j`'s ghost.
- **It cannot be kept in `simInv`.** A clause "every `isAcked γ j t i` has `t ≤ σ.s j.currentTerm`" would have to be re-proved whenever another node's hook closes `simInv`, and there is no authority there to prove it from.
- **It is only used at `j`'s own receive line**, the truncation case of `QuorumLogInv`. That hook holds `j`'s `ownRaftGhost`.

**Repair.** Take it at `j`'s own hook from `AcksTerm`, and drop the row from the per-server table. B1's `histsOk` mirror needs the same care: a snapshot refreshed at `j`'s hooks, tied to the bags.

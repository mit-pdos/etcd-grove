## Second tier

### F1. The leader's new entries need records before the same `Step`'s sends, and raft mints them only at `Step`'s end

**Principle:** "every premise has a source the client sees", checked at the point the premise is
due.

**Evidence.**
- **Raft sends the new entries within the same handler.**
  - A win: `becomeLeader(); bcastAppend()` (`raft.go:1719-1720`).
  - A proposal: `appendEntry(...)` then `bcastAppend()` (`raft.go:1367-1370`).
  - Each `MsgApp` from `pr.Next` carries the just-appended entries: the win's empty entry, the
    proposal's batch.
  - `send` traces at once (`raft.go:593`, `:599`).
- **`simFx` needs records for those entries at that moment.** `simFx` requires `msgFacts` for every
  message in `pendingMessages` (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:390-391`). For an append request, that
  includes `logRecs γ (prev) es` over its entries (`:374`). So each send line's `FxPres` must
  produce the records of entries raft appended moments earlier.
- **Raft's ghost mints those records only at `Step`'s end**, from the handler's `StepCD`:
  - for a win, `ownRaftGhost_step_win` (`Protocol/Refinement.lean:997-1031`), after `stepCandidate`
    returns (`raft__Step/MsgVoteResp.lean:393-470`);
  - for a proposal, `ownRaftGhost_step_propose` (`raft__Step/MsgProp.lean:172-180`,
    `Protocol/Refinement.lean:5355`).
- **The send line cannot mint them itself.** Its fact-keeping premise is persistent
  (`raft.send.tracedSpec` takes `FxPresT`, `TracedHooksDraft.lean:946-949`), so it can mint nothing.
- **The documentation describes a minting point that does not exist.**
  - `srvFacts`' docstring says the pending entries are those "which raft appends (and so records)
    only after the hook" (`TraceSimFacts.lean:279-281`). Raft records them at `Step`'s end, after
    the sends.
  - The plan's split says "the empty entry at the append" (`trace-validation-plan.md:285-286`). But
    no caller holds anything at the append: it is inside `becomeLeader` → `appendEntry`, between
    `traceReplicate` (`raft.go:849`) and `raftLog.append` (`:852`).

**Callers affected.**
- Every send line that carries entries appended in the same call: the win's broadcast, and every
  proposal's broadcast in a multi-node cluster.
- The workload has both (`examples/raft/tracecheck/concurrent.go:105`: 3 nodes, 60 proposals).

**Fix.**
- Mint the records in the `Replicate` line's step, which comes before the append and so before
  every send. Use `FxPresU` (`trCap_fire_any`, one per normal entry through `TraceFireN`):
  - `R` (captured): the term log's authority `ghostMapAuth γt 1 M`, from `leadLogOwn`, or from the
    `BecomeLeader` line's `R'` for the win's entry; the entries' tickets (`entTk`, from `Step`'s
    `Hents`, `raft__Step.lean:654-655`); the time receipt (`Hrc`);
  - inside: `propTk_set` for each ticket (a basic update) and the fragment `i ↪[γt]□ (e, pt)`,
    which give `isEntryR` (`Ghost.lean:1604-1617`). The entry is stamped by then (`raft.go:836-837`);
  - `R'`: the authority with the fragments, and the records.
- The send lines' persistent `FxPres` then only reads the records, and `raft.send.tracedSpec`
  stands as it is.
- Raft's propose and win ghost steps then take records already minted. This is the same kind of
  split as the win's, and it should be recorded beside it in the plan.
- Correct `srvFacts`' docstring (`TraceSimFacts.lean:279-281`) and the plan's split.
- The pending entries of A9 stay as they are. Records minted early do no harm to them.
- Uncertain: I did not check `ev2Rec` (`Ghost.lean:1616`). It concerns an empty `ConfChangeV2`,
  which P3 excludes from the workload.

### F2. The win's split, as the plan records it: where the registration happens, and which clause needs relaxing

**Principle:** "a clause is stated over what its user holds". The brief asks whether each recorded
Prove-phase change is feasible as described.

**Evidence.**
1. **No caller-held point between the poll and the hook knows that the win happens.**
   - The plan puts the registration (`win_register`, `reWins`) "before `becomeLeader`'s hook"
     (`trace-validation-plan.md:284-286`).
   - The only such point a caller holds is in `Step`'s proof, before `stepCandidate`.
   - There the win is not known: `CandVoteRespResultC` gives only `won = true → JQuorum c (granted
     votes')` (`Spec/go_etcd_io/raft/v3/stepCandidate.lean:65-66`).
   - Registering a win that does not happen leaves a registered candidate for good.
2. **The clause that breaks depends on the route, and the plan names neither route's.** Raft's proof
   keeps the ghost at the state of `Step`'s entry until the end. So at `traceBecomeLeader` the
   ghost's role is still `candidate`.
   - On that route `campOwn`'s leader clause (round 5's E4, now in the plan and in
     `TraceSimSpecInv.lean:1369-1371`) is never consulted. Its candidate clause still holds.
   - The term log created in the line's step breaks `TermsOk` instead (`GhostState.lean:410-413`:
     a term log of the current term only at a non-candidate). The registration breaks `WinsOk`, as
     recorded.
   - If instead the ghost's role moved to `leader` before the hook, `leadLogOwn` (`:417-430`: the
     leader's term log present) would break before the line, as well as `campOwn`.
   - If `Step`'s proof keeps `ownLogGhost` open across the handler (taking the term map's and the
     wins' authorities out, and closing once at the end), no clause needs relaxing.

**Callers affected.** `raft.Step`'s `MsgVoteResp` arm at a candidate (`raft__Step/MsgVoteResp.lean:310-470`),
and `stepCandidate`/`becomeLeader`'s traced statements.

**Fix.** Record the route in the plan, and correct `specInv_becomeLeader`'s docstring to match:
- **(a), recommended:**
  - `stepCandidate`'s spec gains the converse, `JQuorum c (granted votes') → won = true`. This is
    true: `poll` returns `VoteWon` exactly on a joint quorum of granted votes.
  - `Step` registers before the call whenever the quorum holds, and lends the `BecomeLeader` line's
    `FxPresU` (term map without `T`) under that condition.
  - The ghost stays open until the end of `Step`. The win's ghost step becomes three lemmas on its
    components: registration, the term log, the entry's record (F1).
- **(b):** `stepCandidate.tracedSpec` takes the registration as a caller-supplied update
  (`|={⊤ ∖ ↑(raftRegN γ)}=>`) that it fires between `poll` and `becomeLeader`. It must give the
  caller's resources back on a loss.
- In either case, if a closed intermediate predicate is wanted, the relaxations are `WinsOk` and
  `TermsOk` for a registered, not yet appended candidate. `campOwn` needs no relaxation.

### F4. `FxPresU` captures its resource: the hook-path statements that thread one between lines need a wand, and the untraced instance has no gate

**Principle:** "one spec per function; special cases are instances"; caller burden.

**Evidence.**
- **A resource created by one line can only reach the next line's `FxPresU` through a wand.**
  - Inside one call to `becomeLeader`, the `BecomeLeader` line's `R'` (the term log) is what the
    `Replicate` line's `FxPresU` must consume (F1).
  - `R'` exists only after the first hook fires, while the caller of `becomeLeader` must supply
    both before the call.
  - So `becomeLeader`'s (and `stepCandidate`'s) traced statement must take
    `R' -∗ FxPresU … R''` for the second line, a wand over a wand.
- **There is no mode gate for `FxPresU`.** `FxPresT` (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:565-569`) gates only
  the persistent form. In an untraced cluster raft's ghost still needs the term log and the records
  that the traced instance obtains through `R'`. Without a gate, the one statement per function has
  no way to give them in that mode.

**Fix.**
- Add the gate `FxPresUT tr γ id E Φ Φ' R'`: `|==> R'` when `tr = none`, `FxPresU …` otherwise. Its
  law is `FxPresUT none … R' ⊢ |==> R'`. Then a hook-path statement takes one premise for both
  modes.
- Optionally restate `FxPresU` with the lent resource explicit (`FxPresU … R R'`, law premise
  `FxPresU … R R' ∗ R`), as round 5 sketched. Chained lines then read
  `FxPresU … R R' ∗ FxPresU … R' R''` without nested wands.

### First tier

**N1. `LeaderCompletenessInv` (checked by `Traceetcdraft.cfg`) is not an invariant of raft, nor of `etcdraft.tla`. A three-voter execution of the harness violates it, so `Main` can panic, and "route (a) from `lc_core`" cannot prove it.**

- **Claim.** The note says `LeaderCompletenessInv` is one of "raft's safety theorems: route (a) from `isCommitted_agree`, `lc_core`, ..." ("The invariants").
- **The invariant** (`tla/etcdraft.tla:859-869`, `Invariants.lean` `leaderCompletenessInv`): for every leader `l` with `currentTerm[l] > entry.term`, `log[l][idx] = entry` holds for every committed entry of every server.
- **Why it fails.** It compares against the entry's *creation* term, while Raft's Leader Completeness is about leaders of terms after the term in which the entry was *committed*. A stale leader of an intermediate term breaks it.
- **The execution** (three voters, no restart, no configuration change):
  1. Node 1 leads term 2. Its empty entry at index 4 (term 2) reaches no one.
  2. Node 2 leads term 3 with node 3's vote, and appends its empty entry at 4 (term 3).
     - Node 1 learned term 3 from node 2's `MsgVote` and refused it, because its log is more up to date.
     - Node 2's entry reaches no one, and node 2 stays leader of 3 (no check-quorum, nothing of term > 3 reaches it).
  3. Node 1 leads term 4 with node 3's vote. It replicates indices 4 (term 2) and 5 (term 4) to node 3 and commits 5. Index 4 (term 2) is now committed.
  4. Leader node 2 has `currentTerm` 3 > 2 and `log[2][4]` of term 3, so the invariant is violated. (A stale leader whose log is shorter makes the check fail evaluating instead.)
- **Witness.** `/tmp/claude-1000/-workspace-etcd-grove/8e48033c-792a-435a-bae3-16d75b0d1a02/scratchpad/r2/tc/witness_lc_test.go`, which drives three `RawNode`s through `Sim`.
  ```
  go test -tags with_tla -run TestWitnessLeaderCompleteness -v .
  events 112: direct invariant LeaderCompletenessInv violated at line 110 porcupine Illegal
  ```
  Line 110 is node 1's `Commit` (commit 3→5). Every earlier line matches. The trace is in `.../scratchpad/r2/lc.ndjson`.
- **Reachable in `Main`.** Every step is one the concurrent harness can take under Goose's semantics:
  - campaigns come from ticks;
  - drops come from `RandomUint64`, isolation, or `Node.Step`'s context timeout;
  - unbounded delays come from the network goroutines.
  - Even with `DropPct = 0` and no isolation, delaying node 2's messages is enough.

  So no premise on the harness's configuration excludes it.
- **Who it affects.** The closed theorem (`traceAccepts` includes the eight invariants) is false. Layer B's plan for the invariants is affected too.
- **Repair** (the user's decision, Q-level):
  - (a) Check a corrected invariant: guard on `currentTerm[l] ≥ currentTerm[i]` (`i` the server whose committed prefix is checked).
    - Every committed entry `i` knows was committed at a term `≤ currentTerm[i]`.
    - So a leader of a term ≥ that one has it: Raft's leader completeness, or it is the committing leader.
    - This holds as far as I can see; I did not prove it.
  - (b) Drop `LeaderCompletenessInv` from the statement's list. The statement then departs from upstream's `.cfg`, and the docstring says so.
  - Either way:
    - record it as item 7 of `notes/raft-trace-validation.md`;
    - consider reporting it upstream: TLC model checking `MCetcdraft` with that invariant should find it at larger bounds;
    - name the route-(a) fact the corrected invariant needs: `lc_core` in its "committed at term `T_c`, leaders of `T > T_c`" form.

**N2. P9 is false: gap 6 arises with three voters. The second `MsgVote` can be the leader's own, delayed past its first heartbeat.**

- **Claim.** P9: "at most three voters ... impossible with three". Also `notes/raft-trace-validation.md` item 6: "a known leader of `T` had the follower's vote: the other voter is the second candidate".
- **The argument's flaw.** It assumes the `MsgVote` comes from a *second* candidate. It can come from the leader itself:
  1. Node 1 campaigns at term 2 and wins with node 2's vote.
  2. Its `MsgVote` to node 3 is delayed. Its first `MsgApp` to node 3 is dropped or delayed.
  3. Its heartbeat reaches node 3 first. Node 3 runs `becomeFollower(2, 1)`: `lead = 1`, `Vote = None`, log unchanged.
  4. The delayed `MsgVote` arrives:
     - raft's `canVote` is false (`lead != None`, `raft.go:1232-1235`), so node 3 rejects;
     - `HandleRequestVoteRequest` grants, because `votedFor = Nil` and node 3's log is not more up to date than node 1's at its campaign.
  5. The trace fails at node 1's receipt of the rejection, or at node 3's next validated line (its `votedFor`).
  - If node 3 had accepted the `MsgApp` first, its last term would be 2 and both sides would refuse. So the window is a heartbeat ahead of both the `MsgVote` and the `MsgApp`, which the harness's per-message goroutines allow.
- **Witness.** `/tmp/claude-1000/-workspace-etcd-grove/8e48033c-792a-435a-bae3-16d75b0d1a02/scratchpad/r2/tc/witness_p9_test.go`
  ```
  events 63: direct trace not matched: no behavior steps through line 63 porcupine Illegal
  line 63: ReceiveRequestVoteResponse nid 1 ... "from":"3","to":"1","reject":true
  in transit: RVResp{3->1 t2 granted=true} x1
  ```
  (`checktrace -explain .../scratchpad/r2/p9.ndjson`). Line 60, node 3's `ReceiveRequestVoteRequest`, follows line 56, its heartbeat receipt, with `vote 0`.
- **Who it affects.** The closed theorem (`DefaultCConfig.Nodes = 3` does not discharge P9). Layer B's P9. The gap list's item 6.
- **Repair** (the user's decision):
  - (a) Make the harness's network FIFO per (sender, receiver) link: one queue goroutine per link, with drops still allowed.
    - A leader's `MsgVote` to `F` is handed out at an earlier `Ready` than any of its heartbeats or `MsgApp`s to `F`, so it is delivered first or never.
    - With three voters, no other `MsgVote` of the leader's term can reach `F` (round 1's argument).
    - P9 then reads "three voters and FIFO links", with D discharging both.
    - This does not help N1.
  - (b) Fix the specification (add `lead` to `HandleRequestVoteRequest`): a different checked spec.
  - Correct item 6's last sentence either way.

### Second tier

**N3. Route (a) needs facts about the other servers of `σ`, which a hook of node `i` does not hold.**

- **Where the facts live.**
  - At node `i`'s hook, the proof owns `simNode γt i` and raft's shared invariants (`raftCommitInv`: committed log, vote and win tables).
  - It does not own the other nodes' `simNode` or their `R_j`, which are held by their run goroutines.
  - Most of the eight invariants quantify over all servers (`MoreThanOneLeaderInv`, `MoreUpToDateCorrectInv`, `LogMatchingInv`, `QuorumLogInv`, the corrected LC).
- **What the plan must add.** "Route (a) ... for the idle nodes" works only if `simAuth`'s invariant carries, for every server `j`, the persistent raft facts its `σ.s j` stands for. For example:
  - `σ.s j` leader of `T` implies `isLeader γ T j`;
  - `σ.s j`'s log entries are raft log records (`logRecs`/`isEntryR` witnesses);
  - `σ.s j`'s committed prefix is committed in `raftCommitInv`.

  Node `j`'s steps maintain them. This is a hybrid of (a) and (b): name it, and list the clause each invariant needs.
- **Truncation states.** At a receive line's truncation states, the receiver's clause is the "strict prefix of a raft log" variant, so S5's lemmas are stated against these clauses.
- **Where the corrected LC fits.** N1's corrected `LeaderCompletenessInv` fits this shape: leader-of-`T` and committed-at-`T_c` facts.

**N4. Increment 2's contract places the simulation step in the logger, which cannot prove it. A weaker logger contract is the stronger spec.**

- **The note's text.** "a logger whose `TraceEvent(ev)` is a logically atomic update `simStep γt i ev` ... given that `ev = eventOf …` ... steps `σ` to its unique successor".
- **Why the logger cannot prove it.**
  - Whether `stepLine` has exactly one successor, and whether the invariants hold there, depend on the hook's phase facts (the handling phase), on other servers (N3), and on the won tally (S7).
  - None of that is in "`ev = eventOf …`", and none is available to the logger (the harness's `SyncRecorder`).
  - Read as a premise the logger's implementation must meet, it is unprovable. Read as the caller's atomic update (TaDa), the hook proves it, and the logger only fires it.
- **What PRINCIPLES favours.**
  - "TaDa, not HoCAP".
  - "A commit point the client chooses is an atomic update the library hands out".
  - Coupling: the harness's logger spec, and through Q1 raft's constructors, would name `σ`, `Φ` and the TLA+ transcription.
- **Suggested restatement.**
  - `isTraceLogger γt tl`: `tl = nil`, or `∀ ev Q, AU⟨∀ L, traceAuth γt L⟩⟨traceAuth γt (L ++ [ev]), Q⟩ -∗ WP tl.TraceEvent(ev) {Q}`. This is generic and names nothing of the specification. `SyncRecorder` proves it with `traceAuth` in its lock invariant.
  - The simulation (`σ`, phantoms, `R`) lives in raft's package as raft's own invariant, keeping `traceFrag γt L` in lockstep with `σ`. Each hook's call site supplies the AU, stepping `σ` inside it.
  - Raft's API exports the law `traceAuth γt L ∗ simInv γt ⊢ ⌜L's prefix steps to a unique state with the invariants⌝`, from which D gets `traceAccepts`.
  - The logger contract then says only what the logger does. Any simulation is a layer on it, so it is the stronger spec.

**N5. The note's increment 2 does not cover three things a proof meets.**

- **(a) The bootstrap lines.**
  - `traceInitState`, `traceConfChangeEvent` and `traceBecomeFollower` are called inside `newRaft`/`Bootstrap` (`raft.go:469, 487`; `bootstrap.go`).
  - At that point `σ` does not exist yet: `σ_init` (`newTrace`'s `serverOf`) depends on every node's last bootstrap line, and `server` on the set of all nids in the final trace.
  - The contract needs a bootstrap mode (append without stepping).
  - `σ` should be allocated after the last `StartNode`, from the recorded prefix, with an invariant that only nodes 1..3 ever log (so `server = {1,2,3}`).
- **(b) The `inFlight` tokens of handed-out messages.**
  - They are created on the run goroutine at `acceptReady`, after the application already holds `rd` (`node.go`: `readyc <- rd` precedes `acceptReady`).
  - So `Node.Ready`'s spec cannot give them. They must reach the application through `Node.Advance`'s post, or through a deposit the application withdraws after `Advance`.
  - `Node.Step`, `RawNode.Step` and `raft.Step` must carry a token for a traced message, gated on the logger. On a context timeout, `Node.Step` gives it back.
  - I could not verify whether Perennial's channel specs let `advancec`'s receiver hand resources to the sender at the rendezvous (uncertain).
  - These are changes to raft's node API: list them with Q1.
- **(c) The receive hook needs the token to be keyed by `msgAbs m`** (a multiset of tokens, since equal messages occur: repeated heartbeats). Say so.

### Third tier

- **T1. The phantoms' argument is false; the conclusion is right.**
  - The note says "A phantom has a lower term than its destination's current term at its creation". A phantom carries the *replier's* term (`RejectAppendEntriesRequest`, `HandleRequestVoteRequest`: `term := v.currentTerm`), which exceeds the stale sender's term. Example: a stale leader `L` of `T` heartbeats `F` at `T+1`; the phantom to `L` has term `T+1`, and `L` is still at `T`.
  - The correct reason: every receive line is of a real handed-out message, whose `msgAbs` is in `messages` (P6); a phantom equal to it gives the same successor.
  - No receive predicate ignores a field in which phantoms and real messages could differ: a rejection's `matchIndex` is 0 on both sides.
- **T2. The bootstrap `ApplyConfChange` "at a leader also `Commit` and `MsgApp` lines" is unreachable.**
  - `hup` refuses to campaign while `hasUnappliedConfChanges()` holds (`raft.go:1001`). `applied` stays 0 until the first `Advance` steps `MsgStorageApplyResp`, and the application applies the three entries before that `Advance`.
  - The four traces I generated have all 9 post-bootstrap `ApplyConfChange` lines at `StateFollower` (`.../scratchpad/r2/an.py`).
  - Either prove it unreachable or keep the case, but do not cite it as reachable.
- **T3. The `Step` table's `MsgAppResp` row.**
  - `Commit` is logged only when `MaybeUpdate` succeeds or the probe shortcut fires (`raft.go:1450`). A stale success logs nothing.
  - A rejection runs `MaybeDecrTo`, possibly `BecomeProbe` and one `MsgApp`, and no `Commit`.
- **T4. Rows to add.**
  - `Propose` at a follower with a leader: it forwards `MsgProp` (untraced; the receiver's run loop overwrites `From`, `node.go`). At a candidate or a leaderless follower, the proposal is dropped.
  - `ReportUnreachable`: no line, but `BecomeProbe` at a leader changes `Next`. It does not change `Match`, which is what `R` reads.
- **T5. `msgAbs` should name its defaults** (`subtype ""` for vote messages, the unused fields 0), since `Msg`'s equality is structural.
- **T6. Stale text in `notes/raft-trace-validation.md`.**
  - Line 35 still says a message is "logged as sent ... before the Ready that hands it to the application, so before its receipt". Round 1's F1 asked for this correction; `concurrent.go` was corrected, the note was not.
  - Item 5's last sentence ("The default configuration ... never ... cuts a batch") is false under Goose's semantics (F3), and moot now.
- **T7. `CommittedIsDurableInv`.** "the leader's own match is set ... to its last index, persisted or not": it is always persisted. The candidate's `Ready` hands out its `MsgVote`s, so it precedes every grant (P8). Saying so is the argument.
- **T8. P5's parenthesis.** "Proposals + 1 + the bootstrap's": a leader appends no bootstrap entries. The bound is 1 + 61 per Replicate period.

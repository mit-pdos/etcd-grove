## First tier (wrong or unusable for the closed theorem)

### E1. The `BecomeLeader` line's `FxPres` is unprovable as `FxPres` is shaped, under any split of the win

**Principle:** "every premise has a source the client sees". The source must also be usable where
the premise is consumed.

**Evidence.**
1. **`FxPres` is persistent and has no update.** It is
   `□ ∀ …, ⌜…⌝ -∗ votesTbl γ -∗ simFx γ C σ -∗ votesTbl γ ∗ simFx γ C σ'` (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:526-535`).
   `trCap_fire_any` takes it as-is (`:674-680`), and so does `trCap_fire` (`:549-553`). The proof
   uses it only once, inside the logger's atomic update (`TracedHooksGlobal.lean:513-514`, `:573`).
2. **The pre-state needs a non-persistent resource.** `becomeLeader_hyps` needs
   `ghostMapAuth γm 1 terms` with `terms !! T = none` (`TraceSimLeader.lean:162-171`; `isEntryR_term_ne`, `:58-60`). That
   authority is the node's own ghost (`GhostState.lean:2697`, held in `ownStepGhost`), and it is
   not persistent.
   - A `□` proposition can only be introduced in a context of persistent hypotheses. So the caller
     cannot use the authority inside `FxPres`.
   - It cannot use it outside either. The hypotheses are about `σ`, which only exists inside the
     simulation invariant.
   - No persistent fact can replace the authority. "No record of term `T`" is invalidated by `i`'s
     own later append, so no persistent resource can imply it.
3. **The post-state needs the term log to exist.** After the line, `i` is a leader in the
   specification's state. So `srvFacts` of the new state requires `leadFull γ T es`
   (`TraceSimFacts.lean:292-293`), with `es = F0`.
   - `leadFull` is `leadPrefix ∗ lpfull` (`Ghost.lean:2930`).
   - `leadPrefix` needs `isTermLog γ i T γt bL l0` (`Ghost.lean:1996-2000`): **`T`'s term log
     must exist.**
4. **The two needs collide.**
   - The pre-state's hypotheses need the term log absent, held in a non-persistent authority.
   - The post-state's facts need the term log present.
   - `FxPres` is one persistent wand with no `|==>` between the two.
   - So neither half of a split can sit before or after the hook and work:
     - term log before the hook: `hfree` is false;
     - term log after the hook (the plan's split): `leadFull` is missing at the `BecomeLeader`
       line, and again at the `Replicate` line of the empty entry.
5. **The recorded Prove-phase item is wrong on this point.** It says "the term log with the empty
   entry at its append" (`notes/trace-validation-plan.md:276`; `TraceSimLeader.lean:15-21`;
   `TraceSimSpecInv.lean:1365-1367`). The term log must exist by the end of the `BecomeLeader`
   line's step. It must be created *inside* that step, after the hypotheses are read, and
   `FxPres` gives no place to do that.

**Caller affected.** `becomeLeader` → `traceBecomeLeader` (`raft.go:978`, after `r.state =
StateLeader` at `:960`, before `appendEntry` at `:980`), through `trCap_fire_any`. This is every
election, so every trace with a leader.

**Fix.**
- **(a), recommended:** a linear, framed variant of `FxPres` and of the law.

      FxPresU γ id E Φ Φ' (R R' : IProp GF) : IProp GF :=
        R -∗ ∀ C cs P e a a' P' σ, ⌜(FxPres's premises)⌝ -∗ votesTbl γ -∗ simFx γ C σ -∗
          |==> votesTbl γ ∗ simFx γ C (withNode σ …) ∗ R'

      trCap_fire_anyU : PhaseStepB … → … →
        trCapB … b Φ Λ ∗ tl ↦□ ∗ FxPresU γ id E Φ Φ' R R' ∗ R ⊢ TraceFire E (trCapB … true Φ' Λ' ∗ R')

  - `FxPres` is the case `R = R' = emp`, so the existing callers do not change.
  - `trCap_fire`'s proof already runs inside a fancy update (the `imod`s at
    `TracedHooksGlobal.lean:583-600`), so the `|==>` costs nothing.
  - At `BecomeLeader`:
    - `R` is the term-map authority without `T` (and the registered win's tokens);
    - inside, `becomeLeader_hyps` gives the pure hypotheses;
    - `T`'s term log is then allocated with initial log `F0` and no entry, which gives
      `leadFull γ T F0` for the post-state;
    - `R'` is the authority with `T`.
  - The split then becomes:
    1. registration (`isLeader`) before the hook;
    2. the term log inside the hook's step;
    3. the empty entry's fragment at the append.
  - Correct the plan, `TraceSimLeader.lean:15-21` and `specInv_becomeLeader`'s docstring to match.
- **(b), the alternative:** derive the freshness from the simulation's own history instead of
  raft's authority. That means a pure clause over the trace `L`: an entry or append request of `T`
  implies an earlier `BecomeLeader` line of `T`, and a candidate's share has none at its term. With
  it, the term log can be created before the hook.
  - It costs a new `simInv` clause and its preservation by every line. That is more work than (a).

## Second tier

### E2. `msgFacts`' commit certificate cannot give the receiver what its docstring says, and nothing uses it

**Principle:** "every premise has a source the client sees"; "after a change, drop what existed
only for what it changed".

**Evidence.**
- What `msgFacts` carries:
  - It carries `∃ L, |L| = m.commitIndex ∗ isCommitted γ L ∗ logRecs γ base0 L`
    (`TracedHooksDraft.lean:373`).
  - `quorumLog_appendEntriesAlreadyDone`'s docstring (`TraceSimSpecInv.lean:2003-2006`) says the
    follower's raised commit gets its `isCommitted`/`commitIn` from that `L`.
- Why it cannot:
  - `srvFacts` needs `commitIn γ es (W64 c) (W64 tm)` (`TraceSimFacts.lean:297`).
  - `commitIn` is `commitJ ∗ dynOn …witsB…` (`Ghost.lean:3267`). `commitJ` needs a bootstrap
    bound or a `cq` (`:3249-3253`).
  - `isCommitted` is only a lower bound of the committed list (`Ghost.lean:857`). No lemma turns it
    into a `cq`.
- Where the receiver's certificate actually comes from:
  - raft's own `isMessage`, which `Step` receives as `#Hmsg` (`raft__Step.lean:679-681`);
  - for `.app`, `commitIn γ (fp ++ l) m.commit m.term` (`GhostState.lean:198`);
  - for `.heartbeat`, `commitIn γ Fl m.commit m.term` (`:216`);
  - `commitIn_move` (`Ghost.lean:3469-3478`) carries it to the follower's log.
- No lemma reads the new conjunct: `grep msgFacts` shows uses only in `TraceSimLeader`, for
  entries and `isLeader`.

**Callers affected.**
- Every send line's `FxPres` for `MsgApp`/`MsgHeartbeat` owes the conjunct, which costs little.
- Every committing receipt's prover is pointed at the wrong source.

**Fix.**
- Drop the conjunct, and point the docstring at `Step`'s `#Hmsg` (`commitIn` there, then
  `commitIn_move`).
- If it is kept so the simulation does not depend on raft's message facts, make it what the
  receiver needs: `∃ F, logRecs γ base0 F ∗ commitIn γ F (W64 m.commitIndex) (W64 m.term) ∗
  isCommitted γ (F.take m.commitIndex.toNat)`.

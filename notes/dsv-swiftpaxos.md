## The kit over remote (what changed against the lab's kit)

- **Orchestrator and model.** The lab ran its own `Remote`: kitgen's `Main`/`ClientNode`, the lab's driver, recorder and `check.KV` over `models.KvIn/KvOut`, and its porcupine axiom. Here `Orch` does three things:
  - `New`: the lab's configuration and leader-change protocol, unchanged;
  - a wait of `StartMs`;
  - `remote.Check` against `Model`.
- **`Model`** is a key-value map with **pending puts**: `State{Cur, Pend}` per key.
  - A put of unknown outcome is pending; it may take effect later, once, or never.
  - A get of `v` is at `Cur[k]`, or at a pending put of `v`, which then takes effect.
  - No distinct values are needed. The model is a simulation of the pending-writes store: each representation stands for the set of configurations `(x, Q)` with `x = Cur ∧ Q ⊆ Pend` or `x ∈ Pend ∧ Q ⊆ Pend − x`, as dsv-paxos's `ModelSet`.
- **`Client(c)`** makes its node before calling `remote.ServeClient` (dsv-paxos's finding). `newClient` waits for the configuration, then starts the leader changes. Client `c`'s stagger (`c·StaggerMs`) is in its first connect.
- **Inputs** are `In{Put, K, V}`:
  - keys `0..NKeys-1`;
  - values random, `"v0".."v999"`, drawn by `Gen` with `primitive.RandomUint64` (remote's `gen` has no operation index).
- **Addresses** are all `127.0.0.1`: node `i` at port `7600+i`, rpc/request ports at `+1000`, configuration ports at `+2000`.

## Design findings

- **`Net/Statement`'s bridge (`robust_of_distBoot`) is for `[netExt]` only.** swiftpaxos hashes (`crypto/sha256`, Perennial's extension), so its machines run over `[netExt, sha256Ext]` (`Dsv.netShaExts`, as the lab's `SafeDistSha`). The bridge's proof is generic in the list, but it is stated for `netExts`/`netGF`. A bridge over the two-extension list is needed: here, in the part, unless `Net/` generalizes it.
- **rpcgen emits an unused import** for a receiver whose package holds no codec type (`*master.Master`). As a result `rpc_gen.go` does not compile; `gen-rpc.sh` drops the import after generating.
- **Wildcard `Listen`** (D21) has no spec in `Net/Spec/net/Listen.lean`, but the fork's replicas and master listen at wildcards.
- **The lab's net/rpc vocabulary** (`RpcWorld`, `RpcCodec`, `kitCodecOk`, `isClient`, `connIface`) differs from `Net/`'s (`CodecSpec`, `codecIs`). The swift proof is written against the lab's in 250+ places, so the hypotheses keep the lab's vocabulary. Copying its definitions (not its proofs) into the part is the next step.
- **gods:** dsv-epaxos and this branch translate it into the same files under `Dsverify/Code/github_com/emirpasic`, and both projects own the directory.
- **Package inits:** the lab's `SwiftNetPkgState` (one class of package-state predicates) needs widening here. It also needs `go/token`, `path`, `net/textproto`, `vendor/.../httpguts`, `crypto/tls`, `internal/bytealg`, `encoding/{hex,json}` and the gods packages, because the net/rpc and net/http models import them and nothing in the imported parts gives them an `IsPkgInit`.

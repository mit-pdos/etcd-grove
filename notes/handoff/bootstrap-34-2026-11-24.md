## Pitfalls

- Kernel: never let the kernel see a definition that unfolds to a huge list (`List.replicate rdSlotsN`,
  `List.range (2^64)`): no `simp only`/`dsimp` with `rfl` lemmas under such a big-sep, use `rw`; define
  such lists by `Classical.choose`. A proof that is fast with `debug.skipKernelTC` but times out in a
  build is this. Never "fix" it with `maxHeartbeats 0` (it ran to 175 GB).
- `bootstrapSnapshot`'s `wp_join`s: the split is on `decide (err = nil)`, so the `nil` branch comes first;
  pass `err`'s points-to into the branches (`with [err]`), and zap's `Error`/`Info` posts bind nothing.
- `srvApplyLoopInit` (`BaseApply.lean`) is destructured positionally by `monitorFileDescriptor` and
  `bootstrap`: adding a conjunct shifts their patterns.
- `etc/gen-link.py` prints to stdout (redirect into the `Link.lean`); `etc/gen-init.py` needs
  `.lake/packages/perennial` at the pin (or `PERENNIAL_DIR`).
- Builds go to the VM (`/shared/tmp/vm/vmlake`); the queue there can be long, and the VM's host may
  change (`/shared/tmp/vm/host`).
- Stage 4 redefines `bootGo.memberJSON` (the JSON model's bytes): the landed bridge lemma is stated over
  `bootGo.ctxs`, so it still builds, but anything evaluating those bytes (the examples' facts) must use the
  new definition.

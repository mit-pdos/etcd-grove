## Design

### The lifecycle facts it rests on

**Entries.**
- Allocated: by the proposer (`node.Propose` node.go:472, `ReadIndex` 615, `confChangeToMsg` 493,
  `RawNode.Propose` rawnode.go:94, `ReadIndex` 556) and `becomeLeader`'s empty entry (961); by
  `appendEntry`'s `proto.Clone` (817), the only place a leader's log entry is made; by
  `stepLeader` for a refused conf change (1341, into `m.Entries[i]`, then cloned); by
  `Bootstrap` (bootstrap.go:61); by `MemoryStorage` (dummies: `NewMemoryStorage` 122,
  `ApplySnapshot` 235, `Compact` 284). A follower's entries are the received message's objects,
  allocated by the application (raft never unmarshals an `Entry`).
- **Written after allocation: only `appendEntry`'s clone, before it is appended** (818-819:
  `cloned[i].Term = new(r.Term)`, `cloned[i].Index = new(...)`), and the composite literals above.
  No other assignment to an entry field exists in the non-test code, and no write through a
  scalar pointer field anywhere (every `*T` field is replaced by a fresh `new(x)`, never written
  through). **Once appended (to `unstable` or `MemoryStorage`), an entry is never written by raft.**
  The one exception is protobuf's hidden state: `proto.Size`/`Clone`/`Marshal` store the
  `sizeCache` atomically and initialize `state` lazily (protobuf v1.36.11
  `internal/impl/encode.go:118-129`), on shared log entries (`entsSize`, `limitSize`).
- Shared: the same `*pb.Entry` objects are in `unstable.entries` (a leader's clones; a follower's
  `m.Entries` objects, `log.go:127`, and in the `fromIndex <= offset` case the message's very
  backing array, `log_unstable.go:209`), in slices the log returns (`unstable.slice` 237 capped,
  `nextEntries` 105 uncapped, `raftLog.slice`), in `MemoryStorage.ents` (`Append` keeps the
  caller's pointers; `Entries` returns them, 162-165), in `MsgApp`s (`maybeSendAppend` 651-658),
  in `Ready.Entries`/`CommittedEntries` (rawnode.go:143-144) and the `MsgStorage*` messages
  (228, 378, 393). The documentation forbids mutating them (storage.go:62-66; `Compact`'s "Entries
  in ms.ents are immutable"), though it points at a `Ready` comment that does not exist.

**Messages.**
- Built fresh with `&pb.Message{...}` and `new(x)` fields at every send site (list in (a)); but
  field *cells* are shared: responses take `To: m.From` (1156, 1165, 1252, 1261, 1789, 1793, 1818,
  1829, 1841, 1845), `Term: m.Term` (1252), `responseToReadIndexReq` `To: req.From`,
  `Entries: req.GetEntries()` (2074-2079), `handleHeartbeat` `Context: m.GetContext()`.
- Written after construction: `send` (`From` 516, `Term` 543) on every message it queues;
  `stepFollower`'s forwarding (`To`, 1720/1739/1761) and `node.run` (`From`, node.go:388) on the
  caller's message; `newStorageAppend{,Resp}Msg` on their own fresh message. **After `send`
  queues a message (592, 598), raft never writes it.**
- Kept after `Step`: `pendingReadIndexMessages` (1366), `readOnly`'s requests (`read_only.go:61`),
  forwarded messages in `r.msgs`, responses sharing the request's cells, a follower's log entries.
- Handed out: `Ready.Messages = r.msgs` (rawnode.go:145) plus `msgsAfterAppend` (165-182);
  `acceptReady` resets both (428-429); `node.run` recomputes and abandons `Ready` values. The
  application may rewrite a message it got from `Ready` (etcd's server replaces a `MsgSnap`'s
  `Snapshot` with the merged one, `server/etcdserver/snapshot_merge.go:51`), so it needs write
  access to the struct.

*R3*: `MemoryStorage.hardState` is `nil` until the first `SetHardState` (`NewMemoryStorage` sets
only `ents` and `snapshot`), so the storage holds a nil-or-read-only hard state (`hsOpt`).

**Snapshots.** Cloned on every path into and out of the log and storage: `unstable.restore` (196),
`nextSnapshot` (114), `raftLog.snapshot` (295), `MemoryStorage.Snapshot` (213),
`ApplySnapshot` (234), `CreateSnapshot` (262); `Ready.Snapshot` is a fresh clone per
`readyWithoutAccept` (156), shared with the `MsgStorageAppend{,Resp}` messages (243, 360). A
`MsgSnap` carries the pointer `raftLog.snapshot()` returned (689). Written in place: by `Ensure*`
(nil fields only; a received snapshot 1836, `ApplySnapshot`'s argument 222) and by
`CreateSnapshot` on `MemoryStorage`'s own (255-261). *R3*: also by `MemoryStorage.Snapshot()` on its
own (`ms.snapshot = EnsureSnapshot(ms.snapshot)`, 212: the field and the nil fields, under the lock)
and by the unlocked `InitialState` on its own conf state (`EnsureConfState`, 132); and
`CreateSnapshot` keeps the caller's `data` slice (261), so the storage's snapshot owns those bytes.
Hence the storage's snapshot is its owner's (`ownMemoryStorage`), not the lock's.

**Hard states** are fresh per `hardState()` (504-510) and never written after construction;
shared by `Ready.HardState`, `prevHardSt` (aliased by `acceptReady`), `MemoryStorage.hardState`
(`SetHardState` keeps the caller's pointer, `InitialState` returns it), the global `emptyState`.
**Conf states**: fresh from `ProgressTracker.ConfState` (tracker.go:148-156), returned through
`switchToConfig`/`applyConfChange`/`RawNode.ApplyConfChange`/`node`'s `confstatec`; read only by
`confchange.Restore`; `MemoryStorage` clones them in (`CreateSnapshot` 259) and returns its own
uncloned from `InitialState`. **Conf changes**: `ConfChange.AsV2` allocates a fresh V2 sharing
`Context`; `AsV1` and `ConfChangeV2.AsV2` return the receiver itself.

### Proposal

**D1. The model's data are pure records, field for field** (*done in R0, final in R1*: Q1). The
protocol model, `Plog`, `CCModel` and the specs' records use `EntryM` (`Term' Index' Type' Data'`),
`MessageM` (the scalar fields: `Type' To' From' Term' LogTerm' Index' Commit' Vote' Reject'
RejectHint'`) and `ConfChangeSingleM` (`Type' NodeId'`) in `Raft37/Proof/.../PbModel.lean`: what
the getters return (nil reads as zero). The records' names stay primed as gogoproto's Goose fields
were, so 19k lines of model port by a rename and `LogState`, `UnstableSt`, `StorState`, `RNState`,
`Msg`, `PState` keep their shapes (`List EntryM`). `absMsg` takes a `MessageM` (it already reads
only scalar fields). The same for a `SnapshotM` (`Metadata.Index/Term`, the `ConfState` lists and
`AutoLeave`, i.e. today's `SnapTrip` plus `Data`) to replace `snapId`/`snapTrip`/`absSnap`'s
`raftpb.Snapshot`, and a `HardStateM` (`Term Vote Commit`) for `hardstateVal`. *R1*: done, the
snapshot nested as the Go messages are: `SnapshotM` (`Data' : List w8`, `Metadata' :
SnapshotMetadataM`), `SnapshotMetadataM` (`ConfState' : ConfStateM`, `Index'`, `Term'`), `ConfStateM`
(the four lists, `AutoLeave'`), so `sv.Metadata'.ConfState'.AutoLeave'` reads as before.

**D2. An entry, once built, is a persistent read-only fact** `entRO (l : Loc) (e : EntryM)`:
`l.Term ↦□ p ∗ cellRO p e.Term'` and likewise `Index`, `Type` (`cellRO p v := p = nil ∧ v = 0 ∨
p ↦□ v`), `l.Data ↦□ e.Data'`, and `pbMsg l` (D5). Justified by C.2 above: raft writes an entry
only between its allocation and its append. Before that, a fresh entry is owned (`appendEntry`
owns its clones and writes `Term`/`Index`; the proposer owns what it builds), and the step
`entRO_persist` freezes it. `[]*pb.Entry` is the slice as before (`↦*□`/`↦*` of `List Loc` plus
`ownSliceCap`), with `[∗ list] l; e ∈ ls; es, entRO l e` beside it: the slice-level reasoning of
the old proof (capacities, `u.entries = ents` adopting a message's array) is unchanged, only its
element type. The data bytes are owned as before (`e.Data' ↦*□ bs`).

**D3. A message is its struct, owned, over persistent cells.** `msgOwn (l : Loc) (dq : DFrac)
(mm : MessageM) (es : List EntryM) (ctx : List w8) ...`: the struct's pointer fields at `dq`
(raft replaces them: `m.To = new(...)`), every scalar cell `cellRO` (cells are shared between a
request and its response, never written), `Entries` as in D2, `Context ↦*□ ctx`, `Snapshot` as
D4's. Queued messages are held at `dq = 1` (an application rewrites them after `Ready`), so
`outboxRel` becomes `[∗ list]` of `msgOwn` over `r.msgs`/`r.msgsAfterAppend` (`List Loc`) with
the same abstraction `absMsg ty mm es ctx`: **no longer persistent**, the main change to
`ownRaftPhys`'s proofs. `Step` (raft, RawNode, node) takes `msgOwn m 1 ...` and the entries'
`entRO` and does not give them back (what the issue `raft-step-writes-caller-message` documents);
a `MsgProp`'s `Entries` slice is owned (`stepLeader` replaces slots, 1341), as today.

**D4. Snapshots are owned objects, cloned at the boundaries.** `ownSnapM (l) (dq) (s : SnapshotM)`
(struct fields, `Metadata` struct, `ConfState` lists, cells). Every clone site gives the caller
full ownership of a fresh copy (`proto.Clone`'s spec); `MemoryStorage` owns its own; a received
`MsgSnap`'s snapshot is owned by `Step` (via D3) so `EnsureSnapshot` may write it;
`ApplySnapshot` takes its argument at `dq = 1` (or the premise that its fields are set).
**Hard states**: immutable after construction (D2's pattern: `hsRO l hs`, persistent), which
covers `prevHardSt`/`emptyState`/`MemoryStorage` aliasing. **Conf states**: fresh ones owned
(`ProgressTracker.ConfState` now returns `csl ↦ cs ∗ cs.AutoLeave' ↦ b ∗` the slices, as R0's
spec does); the one `InitialState` returns is the storage's (premise of `newRaft`: non-nil, read
fraction).

**D5. protobuf's runtime is the trust boundary.** Hidden fields (`state`, `sizeCache`,
`unknownFields`) are split off at allocation into an invariant, `pbMsg l` (persistent), which only
the `proto.*` axioms take (*R1*: `pbMsg T l uf`, one per object, nested objects each their own,
indexed by the unknown fields' bytes because `proto.Equal` compares them); verified code never touches them. New trust-boundary axioms (all of
Go functions of `google.golang.org/protobuf/proto`, names-only, each a spec per message type):
`proto.Clone` (Entry, Snapshot, ConfState: a fresh deep copy, `pbMsg` of the copy), `proto.Size`
(returns some `n`: the specs never fix sizes), `proto.Marshal`/`Unmarshal` (ConfChange,
ConfChangeV2: the codec of `InvConfChangeMsg`, replacing the four gogoproto `Marshal`/`Unmarshal`
axioms), `proto.Equal` (ConfState: replacing `reflect.DeepEqual`; `gogoproto.Equal` goes, since
`LeaveJoint` no longer calls it). The getters, `Enum()`, `Ensure*` are translated and proved.

**What stays**: the protocol model, the ghost state, the refinement's statements and proofs (by
D1's rename); `LogState`/`StorState`/`RNState`/`NState` as records; the specs' pure statements
(which entries, which messages in the model). **What the specs change to**: every physical
premise on a message/entry/snapshot (pointer plus `msgOwn`/`entRO`/`ownSnapM` instead of a Goose
value with slice facts), `Ready`'s fields (nil-or-pointer `HardState`/`Snapshot`, `[]*Entry`,
`[]*Message`), the `Step` ownership transfer, the storage contract (shared entries `entRO`,
`InitialState`'s shared pointers, `Snapshot` fresh), `ApplyConfChange`'s `*ConfState` result.

### Open questions for the reviewer

**Decisions** (the review of R0, applied from R1 on):

- **Q1**: the primed field-for-field records, final (R1 added `ConfStateM`, `SnapshotMetadataM`,
  `SnapshotM`, `HardStateM`).
- **Q2**: `readyWithoutAccept`'s `Ready` is a read-only view of raft's messages; `acceptReady`
  transfers their ownership to the caller (R5).
- **Q3**: `Step` takes the caller's message and keeps it (R4, R5; the issue
  `raft-step-writes-caller-message` documents it).
- **Q4**: the hidden fields in a persistent invariant per object (`pbMsg T l uf`), taken only by the
  `proto.*` axioms, because `proto.Size` writes `sizeCache` on log entries several holders share
  (done in R1; `notes/decisions.md`, "The protobuf runtime as raft's trust boundary").
- **Q5**: `Ensure*` takes ownership where it writes (done in R1: `EnsureConfState`,
  `EnsureSnapshotMetadata`, `EnsureSnapshot` take their argument at `1`).
- **Q6**: the storage contract models `MemoryStorage` (no clone required where it gives none);
  `InitialState` is specified for its single call, at construction (`newRaft`) (R3).
- **Q7**: one swap at the end (R6).
- **Q8**: `RdState.pend` stays R0's rename (`List (MessageM × List EntryM)`).

The questions as R0 put them:

1. **D1's names**: keep the primed field-for-field records (`EntryM`, `MessageM`, ...: cheapest,
   the model text unchanged) or rename to the model's own vocabulary now (a larger diff, once)?
2. **D3, message ownership while queued**: full ownership (needed by an application that
   rewrites a `Ready` message: etcd sets a `MsgSnap`'s `Snapshot`) makes `outboxRel` non-persistent; and
   `readyWithoutAccept` hands out `r.msgs` while raft still holds it (node.go abandons `Ready`
   values): share by fractions until `acceptReady`, or make `readyWithoutAccept`'s `Ready` a
   read-only view?
3. **`Step` taking the caller's message for good** (D3, the new issue): acceptable for
   `isRaftNodeIfc` (etcd steps freshly decoded messages)? The alternative premise ("the caller
   does not use `m` again") is the same thing.
4. **D5**: hidden protobuf fields in an invariant, versus a weaker modelling (ignore them; then
   `proto.Size`'s atomic `sizeCache` store is outside the model). And: one `pbMsg` per message, or
   per message type a deeper `ownProto` that also covers nested messages (`Snapshot.Metadata`,
   `ConfState`)?
5. **`Ensure*` writes** (D4): give `ApplySnapshot`/`handleSnapshot` full ownership, or require
   set fields so `Ensure*` is a no-op (simpler specs, a premise on callers)?
6. **The storage contract** (`isStorage`, `StorageModel`): entries shared read-only with the
   application, `InitialState`'s pointers shared (and written by `EnsureConfState`, unlocked: the
   updated issue), `Snapshot` fresh. Should the contract require a clone where `MemoryStorage`
   does not give one?
7. **Swap granularity**: one swap at the end (R6) as decided, or swap `quorum`/`tracker`/`confchange`
   early (they differ only in imports) to shorten the parallel tree's life?
8. **Model-level theorems with message data**: `RdState.pend : List (MessageM × List EntryM)`
   could become `List Msg` (the abstract message), a simplification the refinement would absorb
   (`readPend` stores `m` itself). Worth doing in R1, or keep R0's rename?

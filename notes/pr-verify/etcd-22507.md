## Verdict

The change is sound and does what it says. It is the same fix as etcd-io/etcd#22585: the two PRs
change `client/v3/leasing/cache.go` identically (same blobs, `6b18084c1fd..a7feb17981a`), and the
fork branches of the two have the same tree (`582fff27e`). So #22585's verification
(`notes/pr-verify/etcd-22585.md`) applies unchanged: every cache entry caches at most one
key-value and its `Count` is their number (`LeaseEntry.countOk`, in the cache's lock invariant),
so a `Get` or a `Txn` get served from the cache after this client deletes the key returns
`Count == 0`, as etcd does. The PRs differ only in their tests; #22507's integration test is a
real regression test (it fails on `main`), but covers only the `Delete` path, not a transaction's
delete.

Not covered: as for #22585, whether the cached key-value itself matches etcd after this client's
own writes (`issues/leasing-update-stale.md`), and the tests themselves.

- **Old spec.** `delete` at a header not older than the entry's drops the cached key-values and
  replaces the header; the count is unchanged.
- **New spec.** The same, and the count becomes 0. It does not hold of the old code, which
  breaks `countOk`.

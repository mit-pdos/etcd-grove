# etcd-io/etcd#7331: ReadIndex may read stale value

- URL: https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/issues/7331 (closed 2017-02-17; labels `type/bug`,
  `area/raft`). Filed by the TiKV authors against etcd's vendored `raft/`.
- Affected: raft as vendored in etcd up to v3.1 (`ReadOnlySafe` read index, introduced in v3.1).
- Fix: PR #7332 "raft: fix read index request for #7331" (commit `9342647e0`, merge `78fbe669a`,
  2017-02-17): `stepLeader` rejects `MsgReadIndex` while the entry at its commit index is not of its
  term. Refined by `3189a66af` (etcd #12680, v3.5; in etcd-io/raft's history): such requests are
  postponed in `pendingReadIndexMessages` instead of dropped, and released by
  `releasePendingReadIndexMessages` after the first commit of the term (the liveness half, see
  [etcd-14027](etcd-14027.md)).

What goes wrong (pre-fix): a newly elected leader's `raftLog.committed` can be below the cluster's
committed index until an entry of its own term commits (Raft §6.4 / §8: the leader learns which
entries are committed only by committing one of its term). `stepLeader`'s `MsgReadIndex` case queued
the request at `r.raftLog.committed` and confirmed leadership by a heartbeat quorum, so the
`ReadState.Index` it handed out could miss entries the old leader had committed and acknowledged:
a linearizable read returns a stale value.

## Classification

**F+ (fixed before the verified code; the spec excludes the buggy version).** The fix is in the
pinned raft: at `509271dac` `stepLeader` postpones the request while `!r.committedEntryInCurrentTerm()`
(`raft.go:1354-1360`). Counterfactually, the proof of the read-index arrival needs exactly this
check: `ownRaftGhost_read_arrive` (`Proof/go_etcd_io/raft/v3/Protocol/ReadIndex.lean:855`) takes
`plogTermAt s.base s.log s.commit = some s.term` as a hypothesis, and its core lemma uses it in the
one case that would otherwise be a stale read. Strong evidence that this is not hypothetical: the
same hypothesis is what exposed the single-voter shortcut, which skipped this check in raft v3.7.0
(`issues/raft-readindex-singleton-stale.md`, fixed in the fork commit the pin includes).

## The counterfactual (step 5)

What a `ReadState` promises: `readStateOk γ st`, i.e. `readOk γ c i` (`Protocol/GhostState.lean:65`,
`:1467`): the global committed log `Lc` at the time the read with context `c` was deposited has
length at most `i`. At the leader, the request's record is created at arrival with index
`s.commit` by `ownRaftGhost_read_arrive` (`ReadIndex.lean:855-860`), whose hypotheses are
`s.role = .leader` and `plogTermAt s.base s.log s.commit = some s.term`. Its core,
`read_arrive_core` (`ReadIndex.lean:752`), splits on the term `t` of the commit that justifies the
current global committed log `P` (`committedJust`):

- `t > T` (a later leader has committed): the record is *doomed* (no quorum will confirm it);
- `t = T`: it is this leader's own commit, at most its commit index (`lcmOwn_bound_keep`);
- `t < T`: an earlier term's commit. Here the hypothesis is used (`HFi`, `:806-808`): the leader's
  entry at its commit index is of term `T`, and committed-log agreement plus term monotonicity of
  the records (`cq_agree`, `recs_mono`, `:839-843`) show that `P`'s commit index `n` cannot exceed
  `s.commit`.

The pre-#7332 code queues the request whatever the term at `s.commit`. In the `t < T` case with
`n > s.commit`, which is exactly the issue's scenario (the old leader committed up to `n`, the new
leader knows only `s.commit < n`), there is no contradiction to derive, `|Lc|` may exceed
`s.commit`, and no `rqRecOk γ s.term ((c, s.commit), d)` can be produced. So the refinement step
`readArrive` (`Protocol/Refinement.lean:4391-4395`, whose rule also carries the `Hct` premise) could
not be taken, `stepLeader`'s `MsgReadIndex` disjunct (which today states the check,
`stepLeader.lean:243-247`) could not be given a model step, and `raft.Step.spec` would fail. **F+.**

The fork's own fix is the same story: upstream v3.7.0 answered at once at a single-voter
configuration *before* the `committedEntryInCurrentTerm` check, reintroducing this bug for
configurations reached by a conf change; the proof could not be completed until the shortcut was
moved after the check (`issues/raft-readindex-singleton-stale.md`).

## Proposal

None (F+). The end-to-end result that covers this bug is `read_notify`
(`Proof/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/etcdserver/read/Results.lean`): `nil` from
`LinearizableReadNotify` only once the applied index reached an index at least the committed index
the caller knew of.

## Findings

None new. Suggested related link for `issues/raft-readindex-singleton-stale.md`: this issue is the
multi-voter original of its first execution (a new leader before its first commit in term).

# etcd-io/etcd#20273: lease not expired when node restart

- URL: https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/issues/20273 (closed 2025-09-10 by the stale bot; label
  `type/bug`). Reported on 3.5.14; a 3-member cluster whose members are killed and restarted in
  turn every 20 s.
- Observed: a 60 s lease, never kept alive, shows its remaining TTL jump back to 59–60 s from time
  to time, and never expires. Expected: it expires 60 s after the grant.
- Fix: none. The maintainer's answer (serathius, linking his comment on #13294): a TTL is counted
  from the last leader change; "TTL for leases is not the exact, but the minimal time for lease to
  be present"; lease checkpointing mitigates it.

What happens, at the pin as on 3.5: when a member becomes leader, `EtcdServer` calls
`lessor.Promote(ElectionTimeout)` (`server/etcdserver/server.go:1960`), which refreshes every lease
(`Lease.refresh`, `lease.go:84`) to `now + extend + getRemainingTTL()`, and `getRemainingTTL` is
the last checkpointed remaining TTL if one was recorded, otherwise the full TTL (`lease.go:76-81`).
Checkpoints are proposed only with the `LeaseCheckpoint` feature, off by default at the pin
(`server/features/etcd_features.go:104`). Each kill of the leader therefore restarts every lease's
TTL; a restart every 20 s of a rotating member kills the leader often enough that a 60 s lease never
expires.

## Classification

**N (works as designed and documented upstream; secondary A-model).** The TTL is a lower bound on
a lease's lifetime, by design; the upper bound the reporter expects holds only with
`LeaseCheckpoint` on, and then only up to the checkpoint interval. On the proof side, nothing here
promises an upper bound either: lease expiry is not modelled (`notes/decisions.md:1476`, "Leases
without liveness": `KeepAlive` promises nothing, "modelling TTLs and expiry" rejected), and the
model has no clock. The verified parts state the reported behaviour exactly where they reach it:
`Lease.refresh` and `Lease.getRemainingTTL` are proved
(`Spec/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/lease/Lease__refresh.lean`, `Lease__getRemainingTTL.lean`:
`remainingTTL` when positive, else `ttl`); `Promote` itself is a trust-boundary axiom (float
arithmetic, `decisions.md`, unit 9), `{{ True }} … {{ True }}` in the server's `lessorOk`
(`Proof/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/etcdserver/InvApplier.lean:396-398`).

## Proposal

None for a bug. The upper bound, where etcd offers one (`LeaseCheckpoint` and
`LeaseCheckpointPersist` on: a lease expires at most a checkpoint interval plus an election timeout
after its TTL, without keep-alives), would be a result of `lease-clock-model` (etcd-15247.md):
needs a clock with bounded drift, `Promote` proved (its float arithmetic needs Perennial's
float-to-integer conversion), the expiry loop and the checkpoint scheduler verified. Large; low
priority, since the behaviour is the intended one.

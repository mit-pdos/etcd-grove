# etcd-io/etcd#17855: Panic occurs when etcd (ver 3.5.13) new node joins the cluster

- URL: https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/issues/17855 (closed 2024-04-23, completed, by ahrtr, with
  no comment; `type/bug`)
- Affected: 3.5.13. The code is unchanged on `main`. Fix: none.

What goes wrong: node1 is started alone and becomes leader. node2 is added (`ConfChangeAddNode`
`4a62c02900b8e042`, at 17:17:58 in node1's log) and started with `--initial-cluster-state
existing`. "Occasionally" node2 panics: `tocommit(21) is out of range [lastIndex(0)]`, which is
`raftLog.commitTo` from `handleHeartbeat`. Only node1's log is attached. It shows node1 losing
leadership at 17:18:00 (two voters, node2 not yet answering) and pre-voting until the streams to
node2 come up at 17:18:05. Low reproducibility; the setup is a test harness
(`cases.rebalance_mock`).

## Classification

**A-model (secondary O-path; diagnosis unsettled).** Same mechanism as `etcd-21178.md` and
`raft-197.md`. A heartbeat carries `Commit = min(pr.Match, committed)` (`raft.go:702` at the raft
pin). The leader resets every peer's `Match` to 0 when it takes office (`reset`, `raft.go:797`), and
a newly added peer starts at `Match = 0` (`confchange.go:262`). `Match` rises only on that peer's
non-rejecting `MsgAppResp` in the leader's current term (`raft.go:1519`). A heartbeat with commit 21
to a process with an empty log therefore means that some process running under node2's ID
acknowledged index ≥ 21 in that term and that this process does not have the entries: a restart
with a wiped directory, or two processes sharing the ID. Neither is possible under the proof's
premises. The raft spec excludes the panic (`raftLog__commitTo.lean:46`, discharged by
`heartbeat_commit_le_last`, `raft__Step.lean:204`), given one ghost per ID minted once
(`rnInit`, `NewRawNode.lean:90`) and no lost durable state. The truncated log does not show which of
the two happened. The harness's restarts of node2 are the likely cause. etcd's join path is not
verified (`bootstrapExistingClusterNoWAL` not translated; `newRaftNode`'s `RestartNode` branch out
of scope): O-path.

A bug in which an honest node acks an entry it then does not have, for example a follower sending
`MsgAppResp` before its WAL `fsync`, would also fit. At the pin etcd's `raftNode.start` sends a
follower's messages after `storage.Save`, and the raft side's justification of acks by the in-memory
log is the known gap that `raft-durable-acks` closes. No evidence in the report points there.

Source: `verified.txt` (scratchpad inventory at `d606c9cac`).

## Proposal

`raft-durable-acks` → `crash-restart-model`, and `join-fresh-identity` (see `etcd-21178.md`).

# etcd-io/etcd#14758: Attached keys deleted before lease expired

- URL: https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/issues/14758 (closed 2023-01-18 after the reporter could not
  reproduce it; labels `type/bug`, `release/v3.5`). Reported on etcd 3.5.4, a single member,
  client v3.5.5.
- Observed: a lease kept alive by `KeepAlive` still exists (`lease timetolive`: TTL 300s, 231s
  remaining) but its attached keys are gone; the keys were deleted "at random", with no other
  writes to them or to the lease. Expected: a live lease keeps its keys.
- Fix: none merged. In 2026-02 PR #21389 (closed unmerged by the stale bot, 2026-06-19) claimed a
  reproduction with two failpoints and a fix (close `revokec` at the start of `Revoke`, check it
  in `Renew` under `le.mu`).

The diagnosis is unsettled. The maintainers ruled out the two documented causes (a delete, a put
without `WithIgnoreLease`), suspected the non-linearizable lease API (#13915), and closed it. The
race of #21389 is real and present at the pin: `lessor.Renew` (`server/lease/lessor.go:396`)
checks `l.expired()` under `le.mu.RLock` (`:415`), drops the lock, and may wait (in the
checkpointer `le.cp`, `:437`, a raft proposal; or the scheduler); meanwhile the lease expires, the
leader's expiry loop proposes its revocation, and the apply thread's `lessor.Revoke` (`:326`) drops
`le.mu` (`:337`) and deletes the lease's keys (`:350`). `Renew` then takes `le.mu` (`:442`), still
finds the lease in `leaseMap` (it is removed only at `:355`, after the deletions), refreshes it and
returns a positive TTL. `revokec`, which `Renew` would have seen, is closed only when `Revoke`
returns (`defer close(l.revokec)`, `:335`). That race gives a keep-alive answer "alive for TTL"
for a lease whose keys are being deleted, but the lease is gone right after; it does not by itself
give the reporter's later observation (the lease present 70 s later without keys).

## Classification

**O-fn** (diagnosis unsettled; secondary A-model). For the one concrete candidate (#21389's race,
reproduced at the pin, below), the faulty functions are unverified: `lessor.Renew` is names-only
(`Code/go_etcd_io/etcd/server/v3/lease.toml:16-19` translates neither it nor the expiry loop),
`lessor.Revoke` is translated but unproved (`notes/decisions.md:5451`, five obstacles), and the
server's `LeaseRenew` and the expiry loop `EtcdServer.revokeExpiredLeases` are untranslated or a
names-only axiom. Even with them verified, what the user relies on ("a renewal returning TTL > 0
keeps the keys for TTL seconds") is a timing property the model has no clock for; the closest
safety property is stated below. The reporter's own observation is unexplained (no reproduction;
it may be N).

Source of verification states: the `Spec/`, `Proof/` and `Code/` files (`verified.txt` not
produced in this batch).

## The argument (step 4)

No spec here is about `Renew`, so none excludes the race. The parts that are verified do not
either: `isApplyLessor`'s `Revoke` clause is atomic for the apply thread and the store (the
deletions and the lease's removal are in one store write transaction, `txn.End()` after
`delete(le.leaseMap, …)`, so a KV reader never sees the keys gone and the lease present), but it
says nothing of a concurrent `Renew`, which reads `leaseMap` directly. `Lease.refresh` is proved to
set `expiry` from the clock; nothing relates `expiry` to a revocation in progress.

What the user expects is a time property: after a `KeepAlive` answer with TTL `t` at time `T`, the
keys stay until `T + t` (or the client learns otherwise). Perennial's model has no real-time clock
(`time.Now` returns an arbitrary value), and the KV model does not represent expiry
(`notes/decisions.md:1476`, "Leases without liveness"; the T3 plan, `decisions.md:6621`: "Leases may
expire at any time", expiry revocations authorised by any client). A clock-free property that does
exclude the race: *`Renew` returns a positive TTL only if, at its linearization point (under
`le.mu`), no `Revoke` of the lease has started.* That is #21389's fix (`revokec` closed under `le.mu`
at `Revoke`'s start, checked by `Renew` under `le.mu`).

## Reproduction at the pin

`go test ./lease/ -run TestRenewDuringRevoke` (a unit test in `server/lease`, written for this
triage, in `issues/etcd-lessor-renew-during-revoke.md`) fails deterministically on `107edf7e4`:
"Renew returned TTL 1 while Revoke was deleting the lease's keys; after Revoke, Lookup = <nil>"
(go1.27.2, 3 of 3 runs).

## Proposal: `verify-lessor-renew-revoke`

Prove `lessor.Revoke` (its five obstacles, `decisions.md:5451`: a share of `le.rd`, the range
deleter's contract from `SetRangeDeleter`, a reader slot per lease for `Keys()`, the batch lock, the
item-set/store coupling; plus `revokec`'s close right) and `lessor.Renew`, with a ghost "revoking"
set in `lessorMu` (a lease enters it under `le.mu` when `Revoke` starts):

```lean
-- Renew: success only for a live lease not being revoked, at the point it holds le.mu
{{ lessorInv lp γl ∗ … }} le.Renew(id)
{{ (ttl : w64) (err : GoInterface), RET (ttl, err);
   ⌜err = nil⌝ → ∃ L R, lessorAt γl L R ∗ ⌜id ∈ L ∧ id ∉ R⌝ (at the linearization point) ∨ … }}
```

which the pinned code fails (the proof of `Renew`'s second critical section cannot show `id ∉ R`).
Cost: medium to large (`Revoke` is the bulk: the design changes across mvcc's and the txn package's
statements listed in decisions.md; `Renew` small once `Revoke`'s invariant exists, plus the
checkpointer's contract). The time property itself needs `lease-clock-model` (etcd-15247.md).

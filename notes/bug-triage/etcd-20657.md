# etcd-io/etcd#20657: Race condition between snapshot cleanup and application

- URL: https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/issues/20657 (closed 2025-09-14; `type/bug`; a maintainer
  answered "a known issue", pointing to #18055).
- Affected: 3.5.18, found by a fault-injection tool. During a leader change the new leader sent
  snapshots 17 and 18 back to back (very low `--snapshot-count`). The follower logged "applying
  snapshot 17" and then raft restored 18. `raftNode` "applied incoming Raft snapshot" 17 and 18,
  `ReleaseSnapDBs(18)` deleted `0000000000000011.snap.db`, and `applySnapshot(17)` panicked in
  `OpenSnapshotBackend`.
- Fix: none merged (see #18055).

## Classification

**A-pre (duplicate of etcd#18055; secondary O-fn).** It is the same bug, reported on 3.5. The 3.5
code differs from `main` (gogoproto `raftpb`), but the race is the same: on 3.5 too `applySnapshot` waits
`notifyc` and then `s.bemu` before opening the file. At the pin `107edf7e4`: `raftNode.start` releases older `.snap.db` files (`raft.go:281`) while
`applySnapshot` has received `notifyc` but not yet opened its file (`server.go:1037-1048`). These
branches are verified only under the no-snapshot premise of the server's `raft.Node` contract
(`InvRaftNode.lean:190`, `:682`; `EtcdServer__applySnapshot.lean:30`). The full argument is in
`notes/bug-triage/etcd-18055.md`. The log shows the exact interleaving the proposal's resource
spec would forbid: the second `Release` runs while `applySnapshot` holds the first snapshot.

Source of verification states: `verified.txt` (this batch).

## Proposal

`server-apply-snapshot` (as etcd#18055).

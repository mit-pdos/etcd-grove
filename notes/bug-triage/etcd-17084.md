# etcd-io/etcd#17084: [3.4.18] panic: page xxx already freed

- URL: https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/issues/17084 (closed not planned, 2026-06-05; `type/bug`,
  `area/bbolt`, `data/corruption`, `stage/tracked`)
- Affected: v3.4.18 (split from #14211). One member of three panicked twice in bbolt ("page xxx
  already freed", the freelist) under heavy load. After a restart it served data that differed from
  the other two: 5 `netmaster` pods instead of 3, and a bolt revision history divergent from
  `{main:1054916837}`, with the same raft and applied index. `bbolt check` passed, and
  `experimental-initial-corrupt-check` was off.
- Fix: none. Never reproduced. fuweid (2025-08) suggested the defrag/`applySnapshot` race of #20271
  as a possible cause without logs to confirm it. Closed because nobody can reproduce it on a
  supported version.

## Classification

**O-pkg (bbolt's freelist and page management); secondary A-model; one candidate cause F−.** The
panic is in bbolt's freelist (`freelist.free`), which is not translated. The backend proofs assume
bbolt's key-value contract (`Spec/go_etcd_io/bbolt/*_kv.lean`), under which committed data is
never lost and no page is double-freed. The diverging member is the state that the server-level
statements here would exclude: the apply thread's backend equals the effects of the applied entries
(`backend-commit-saves-ci`'s `persistedOk`, and the apply loop's `loopOk`). They exclude it only if
bbolt keeps its contract and only across runs without crashes. Here, a panic and a restart happened.
The diagnosis is unsettled, so each candidate cause is classified:

- a bbolt freelist bug or a page stomp: **O-pkg** (bbolt internals), with the proposal
  `bbolt-kv-contract` (`etcd-15498.md`);
- the defrag/`applySnapshot` race of #20271: **F−**. It is fixed at the pin (`581b0b577`, an
  ancestor of `107edf7e4`), and the existing specs admit the buggy version because of `rnApP`'s
  no-snapshot premise and the untranslated defrag (`etcd-20271.md`). Proposal `server-apply-snapshot`;
- a crash (the bbolt panic) losing or tearing a commit: **A-model**, no crash semantics
  (`crash-restart-model`, `fs-crash-model`).

The detection that would have stopped the divergence (the periodic and initial hash checks,
`EtcdServer.monitorKVHash`, assumed; mvcc `hash.go`, 1 of 9 functions verified) is outside the
verified code. Its spec gap is `model-kv-hash`.

Source of verification states: `verified.txt`.

## Proposal

`bbolt-kv-contract` (primary; see `etcd-15498.md`). The other candidates are covered by
`server-apply-snapshot` and `fs-crash-model`.

None new.

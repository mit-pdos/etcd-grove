# Track: shared list/tree lemma library with list-segment automation

## What it is

- **`BtreeLib/ListAuto.lean`** (+ `ListAuto/Attr.lean`): the tactic **`list_seg`** for pure
  side goals about lists, slices, lengths, indices, word bounds and sortedness, plus
  `sorted_seg` and a small library of generic slice-shape lemmas (`seg_*`: insert-at lookup,
  `set` then `take`/`drop`, `take i ++ l[i] :: drop (i+1)`, `P ++ x :: Q` pieces, `Sorted` of
  pieces, slice-header lengths `(slice.slice s V lo hi).len = hi - lo`).
  Pipeline: drop Iris hypotheses; evaluate word literals (`sint.nat (W64 0)` → `0`); unfold
  `subslice`, `Sorted`, slice headers; add `StrictOrder` fields as facts; **prune the context
  to the hypotheses connected to the goal** (proof-mode contexts have 50+); then, each stage
  under a heartbeat budget: `omega` → `grind` (+ conditional `seg` lemmas) → `@[seg]`/`@[list_simp]`/
  `@[len]` simp normalisation with `omega` discharger, then `grind` → `word` → pointwise
  `List.ext_getElem?` + case split → unfold the model (`@[seg_model]`: `lookup`, `ascRange`,
  `countLt`, `Tree.Shape`, `RootBounds`, `removeOp`, ...) and retry; finally `grind` on the
  full context. It closes the goal or fails leaving it unchanged; never `sorry`.
  Extensible: `attribute [seg] myLemma`, `attribute [seg_model] myDef`.
- **`LEMMAS.md`** (generated by **`etc/lemma-index.py`**): 572 lemmas (btree `Lemmas/*`,
  `NodeLemmas`, `Tree`, `Defs`, `Fractional`, `ListAuto`, Perennial `Std/List*.lean`), each with
  full statement, file:line, section, `pure`/`iris`/`simp` tags, grouped into 13 topics
  (sortedness, lookup, insert/delete model, ranges, `countLt`, shape, in-order contents,
  subslice, take/drop/set, index/length, ...), names qualified (`InsertLemmas.foo`), plus a
  **duplicates** list (≈40: e.g. `countLt_le` ×3, `sorted_mid` ×3, `elemsAux_append` ×2).
- **`etc/seg-eval.py`** + `BtreeLib/ListAuto/Probe.lean` (evaluation only): `seg_probe` runs
  `list_seg` (and `omega`) on the current goal, logs result/time/goal, restores the state.
- `./mk` compiles the modules into `build/` (`./check` now has `build/` on `LEAN_PATH`).

## Usage (paste into an agent brief)

```
Pure list/arith side goals: `import BtreeLib.ListAuto`, then try `list_seg` first.
It closes goals about take/drop/set/++/map/replicate/dropLast/getElem?/length/subslice,
slice lengths, insert-at/remove-at index arithmetic, `sint.nat`/`sint.Z`/`W64` bounds (it
calls `word`), `Sorted` of pieces of sorted lists, and simple `lookup`/`ascRange`/`Shape`
facts, using the hypotheses in context (Iris hypotheses are ignored). Works on a pure goal
anywhere, e.g. after `ipureintro`, in `have h : … := by list_seg`, or for `wp` bound side
conditions instead of `word`/`omega`. Missing fact? `list_seg [h, lemma]`.
It fails in ≤ ~15 s with the goal unchanged; then the goal needs a real argument
(induction over a tree/model definition, an existential witness, a btree lemma).
Before writing a lemma: `grep -n '<function or name fragment>' LEMMAS.md` (statements,
file:line, duplicates). Build: ./mk ; LEAN_PATH must include build/ (./check does).
```

## Validation

All numbers from `etc/seg-eval.py` on the non-challenge proofs (33 files with probe points)
and lemma files; see `eval/run4/report4.txt` (full list of failures with goals).

**Pure goals harvested from `Proof/*.lean`** (probe before every `word`/`omega` call, at the
start of every `have … := by` block, after every `ipureintro`; 803 pure goals, Iris goals
skipped):

| probe point | goals | `list_seg` closes | `omega` alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| `word` call sites | 237 | 237 (100%) | 43 |
| `omega` call sites | 133 | 133 (100%) | 133 |
| `have … := by` blocks | 190 | 174 (92%) | 33 |
| after `ipureintro` | 243 | 204 (84%) | 51 |
| **total** | **803** | **748 (93%)** | 260 |

No goal is closed by `omega` but not by `list_seg`. Time per successful call: median
0.39 s, p90 5.3 s, p95 10 s (contexts of 50–90 hypotheses; most of the tail is `word` and
`grind` in the biggest contexts). These numbers are for the version without per-stage
budgets (run4), where a *failure* cost 30–45 s. The delivered version budgets each stage
(`word_bounded`); re-measured on the 31 files other than node__iterate/node__remove
(`eval/run5/`; its node__iterate/node__remove files are copies from run4): 381/405 closed
(94%, vs 393/405 unbudgeted), success median 0.36 s / p90 2.7 s / max 16 s, failure median
4 s / max 23 s.

Failures (55), by category: model predicates needing btree lemmas or induction
(`Tree.Shape`/`RootBounds`/`RemPost`/`GrowMid` of non-literal trees, `elemsAux`/`chElems`
sublists, `ascStart`/`descStart`, `ascRange`/`descRange` of pieces) ≈ 40; list prefixes
`acc <+: pre ∧ …` over ranges (6); goals with metavariables / `#(decide ∃…)` value equalities (8);
one index goal through `sint.nat (i + W64 1)` with `P ++ c :: d :: Q` (node__remove:681).

**Replacing existing proofs** (`etc/seg-eval.py replace`, output in `eval/replaced/`): in
8 proof files, every `have … := by <block>` whose probe succeeded was replaced by
`:= by list_seg` (63 blocks). `items__pop`, `items__truncate`, `BTreeG__ReplaceOrInsert` (9
replacements) compile unchanged. In `min`, `node__get`, `max` and `node__insert`, one block
each fails under the per-stage budgets (it had succeeded unbudgeted). `max`/`node__insert` also hit
the *declaration's* 200000-heartbeat limit, because `list_seg`'s heartbeats count against the
enclosing proof: a proof with 10–20 calls needs `set_option maxHeartbeats 400000 in`.
`BTreeG__deleteItem` failed because the replacement script cut the wrong block (a bug in
the script, not in the tactic). So ≈ 59 of 63 replacements hold.

**Lemmas** (pure lemma statements of `Lemmas/*.lean`, NodeLemmas, ListLemmas restated as
`example … := by list_seg` before each theorem; 175 statements): **68 proved (39%)**,
median 0.5 s. Proved: all the generic list/sortedness ones (`removeAt_list`,
`getElem?_insertAt`, `getElem?_split_left/right`, `split_bounds_left/right`, `set_mid*`,
`take_drop_mid*`, `drop_set_self`, `drop_eq_cons_of_getElem?`, `sorted_*`, `Sorted.sublist`,
`lookup_*` over `++`/`::`, `ascRange/descRange_append/cons`, `delete_append`, `countLt_le`,
`remIdx_*`, `removeOp_nil/sublist`, `shape_zero/succ/child/set_child`, order facts
`ins_asymm`, `not_lt_of_lt`, `gt_of_not_lt_ne`, ...). Not proved (107): 64 need induction over
a recursive model/tree definition (`insert`, `elemsAux`, `pre`/`post`/`chElems`), 10
`countLt`/start-position search (induction), 12 shape/postcondition predicates with
quantified children, 11 range/lookup facts needing sortedness arguments, 5 existential
witnesses (`rm_split1`, `elemsAux_item`, ...), 5 other. `list_seg` does not do induction or
invent witnesses: those lemmas stay (and are indexed in LEMMAS.md).

## Limitations

- No induction, no existential witnesses; model-level facts need the project's lemmas
  (pass them: `list_seg [elems_decomp …]`).
- Heartbeats count against the enclosing declaration: many calls in one proof may need
  `set_option maxHeartbeats 400000 in`.
- Slow in the largest proof-mode contexts (p95 10 s even on success) — the cost is mostly
  `grind`/`word` over 50–90 hypotheses; pruning helps but `word` still sees everything.
- `grind` is noisy in this environment (Iris registers `grind` lemmas) — harmless.
- `ListLemmas.lean` is empty in this copy and `Model.lean`/`Tree.lean` define what
  `ListAuto` imports, so their lemmas were not restated.

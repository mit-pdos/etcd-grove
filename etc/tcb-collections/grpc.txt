# Phase 7's family (notes/phase7-endstate.md): what gRPC, its transport (net/http) and their leaves
# contribute to the top-level theorems. "gRPC is done" is this baseline empty: no axiom and no `sorry`
# of the family under any top-level result (the grpc-specific premises of the top-level statements,
# which a trust-base report does not see, are listed in notes/phase7-endstate.md's section 5). What
# stays trusted then is only the `models/grpc` Go model and the net extension's semantics.
#
#   etc/tcb-report.py --collection etc/tcb-collections/grpc.txt            what it rests on
#   etc/tcb-report.py --collection etc/tcb-collections/grpc.txt --check    against the lines below
#   etc/tcb-report.py --collection etc/tcb-collections/grpc.txt --check --update
#
# `roots PATTERN`: every etcd and Kubernetes example's result (the single-process theorems, the
# drivers' linked `Main`s, putget's distributed `putget_safe_of_*`) and every top-level robustness
# theorem of every tree (mvccfull among them).
roots ^Proof/github_com/mit_pdos/etcd_grove/examples/(etcd|k8s)/
roots TopLevelRobustness\.lean$
# `axioms PATTERN`: the family, by declaring module or name (etc/tcb-report.py --help): grpc and its
# subpackages (status, metadata, peer, credentials, codes, health, grpclog), the interceptors
# (go-grpc-prometheus/providers, otelgrpc and otel's attribute init), net and net/http with their etcd
# leaves (rafthttp's, probing, transport, httputil, types.NewURLs, leasehttp), the bridge, the start's
# address rights, etcd's grpc-facing client and server leaves, and the per-type registrations. Not
# in it: the protobuf, prometheus-metric, zap, context and reflect specs (their own tracks), and
# raft's and rafthttp's message registrations (`pbEntryC_reg`, `pbMessageC_reg`). The family's
# hand-written opaques (`grpcCliPI`, `protoEncodes`, `grpcRepliesFit`, net/http's `Boundary.lean`, ...)

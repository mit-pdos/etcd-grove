# putget_safe (examples/etcd/dist/putget, notes/putget-dist-plan.md): etcd's first distributed theorem,
# one member machine and one client machine, no crash: `theorem putget_safe : putget_safe.spec`
# (Proof/.../examples/etcd/dist/putget/TopLevelRobustness.lean), no hypothesis. Its cross-machine steps
# rest on the four interim axioms of notes/putget-dist-plan.md §12 and notes/etcd-dist-plan.md §18 (the table
# `etcd_dist.etcdSvc`, `etcd_dist.Serve_dist.wp_proof` and the clientv3 contracts
# `etcd_dist.KV_Put_dist.wp_proof`, `etcd_dist.KV_Get_dist.wp_proof`, at the cluster's view, O1 option B,
# 2026-11-25: until then `Client_Put_dist`/`Client_Get_dist` were axioms over a published view, now theorems;
# since the S2 interface switch the address fact is grpc's `grpcAddrAt` and `etcdAddr_set` a theorem),
# retired by S2's `etcdSvc` and proofs. The rest is the
# single-process member's and client's trust base. Baseline written by --check --update after a full
# VM build.
#
#   etc/tcb-report.py --collection etc/tcb-collections/putget-dist.txt [--check [--update]]

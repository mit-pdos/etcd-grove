# The old bbolt proofs (not built)

The specs and proofs of bbolt (v1.5.0, etcd's dependency) from etcd-grove branch `bbolt`, kept
for reference while they are redone on `lean`. They are broken against the current tree: they
were written against `lean` of 2026-10-10 (`67c6b92`) and Perennial branch `goose-bbolt`
(`793297d`), before the raft v3.7 retarget, the module boundaries of `etc/api/`, and the many
Perennial bumps since. Nothing reaches this directory: the `lean_lib` globs of `lakefile.toml`
and the scripts of `etc/` (the roots of `etc/proof_roots.py`, `etc/gen-init.py`,
`etc/api-check.py`, ...) all start from `Code/`, `Spec/`, `Proof/` and the part roots.

**What is here.** The paths below `old/bbolt/` are the branch's own paths, as of its last state:

- `Spec/`, `Proof/`: every spec and proof the branch added (bbolt's root package,
  `internal/common`, `internal/freelist`, and the standard-library specs it needed: `os`, `sort`,
  `sync.Pool`, `log`, `hash/fnv`, `syscall`, `x/sys/unix`, ...), and its versions of the bbolt
  files that predate it (`Proof/go_etcd_io/bbolt/internal/common/{Init,Results}.lean`). Some of
  these names exist on `lean` now with different content (`Spec/os/`, `Spec/sort/`,
  `Proof/go_etcd_io/bbolt/`): `lean`'s are the current ones.
- `Code/**.toml`: the branch's Goose configuration of bbolt and `x/sys/unix`.
- `etc/link-lean-first-bbolt*.txt`: the link orders of `etc/gen-link.py --bbolt`,
  `--bbolt-freelist`, `--bbolt-root`.
- `branch-tooling.patch`: the branch's changes to `etc/goose.py` (the bbolt packages translated
  with bodies), `etc/gen-link.py` (the three bbolt link modes), `etc/tcb-baseline.txt` (bbolt's
  model axioms), `lakefile.toml` and `lake-manifest.json` (the `goose-bbolt` pin), relative to
  `67c6b92`.
- `notes/`: `bbolt-proof-plan.md` (the plan, including the spill design), `bbolt-unsafe.md` (the
  models of bbolt's unsafe code), and `STATUS-bbolt.md` (the branch's bbolt section of
  `notes/STATUS.md`, 2026-10-12: what was verified, coverage, the models, the TCB).
- `wip/`: the branch's own out-of-build directory.

The last commit before the move, `cd1bf36f2e`, also saves the spill proof that was left
uncommitted in the branch's worktree on 2026-10-19 (`Bucket.spill`, `node.spill`, `node.split`
and the `Spill*.lean` lemmas), unchecked.

**What is not here.** The generated translations the proofs were checked against (`Code/` and
`GeneratedProof/` of bbolt, `x/sys/unix`, and the rest regenerated by the branch's Perennial):
`lean` has its own bbolt translation. The branch's history is merged, so they are in it:
`git show cd1bf36f2e:Code/go_etcd_io/bbolt.lean`, and likewise every file at its original path
(`git log --follow old/bbolt/<path>` follows a file back through the move).
